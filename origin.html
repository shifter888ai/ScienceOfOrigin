Fundamental subject

Origin

The fundamental source is the central subject of Science of Origin.

In the developing framework of Science of Origin, Origin is understood as both the underlying Infinity and the ultimate Originator.

Origin is not considered merely as a beginning in time. It concerns the fundamental source underlying existence and origination.

Current foundational positions

  1. Origin is the underlying Infinity.
  2. Origin is the ultimate Originator.
  3. Nothing can go beyond Infinity.

These are foundational positions in the developing framework. Their meaning, implications and relationship to investigable phenomena remain subjects for careful exploration.

Origin as a research subject

Science of Origin does not impose a predetermined limit on its research scope. The nature of Origin, its relationship with origination, and the manifestations arising through origination remain subjects for exploration and investigation.

Open questions

  • What is the fundamental nature of Origin?
  • What is the relationship between Origin and Infinity?
  • How does Origin relate to origination?
  • Can any properties or manifestations of Origin be investigated?
  • What can be established, and what remains unknown?
Research status: Foundational positions and open questions. This page does not present these positions as independently established scientific findings.
