Origin

Investigating the fundamental source of existence and origination: the Oneness Origin from which manifestations arise.

Origin as the indefinable Ultimate Source

Science of Origin investigates Origin not merely as a beginning in time, but as the fundamental source underlying everything that exists and every process of origination. Origin is the Ultimate Source, not an object among other objects or an event at the beginning of a timeline.

Origin is undefinable. This does not mean that Origin is unknowable, undiscoverable or beyond description and understanding. Undefinability does not impose a boundary on inquiry; it means that no definition can contain Origin without limiting what is being described. The formulation “we do not know what Origin is, even Origin does not know what Origin is” expresses undefinability, not ignorance or a limit on discovery. Infinity is a nature of Origin, not Origin itself. Treating Origin as identical to Infinity would turn one nature into a definition of Origin.

True Origin — the Oneness Origin

The term True Origin refers to the Oneness Origin (一體本源). It expresses the foundational position that there is one ultimate Origin, rather than multiple independent ultimate sources.

In this framework, Origin has no separate identity apart from the manifestations associated with it. The apparent multiplicity of things does not, by itself, establish the existence of multiple ultimate origins or an absolute separation from the source.

The distinction between True Origin and the constructed imitation called Fake Origin is examined in Origin Models.

Descriptive perspectives on Origin

Five natures or aspects have been named in the current account. They are not separate entities, a hierarchy, a sequence or an exhaustive list:

  • Infinity: a nature of Origin, not a definition of Origin.
  • Spirit: the perspective of nature, and the closest description of the Source's nature.
  • Consciousness: the perspective of attributes.
  • Freewill: the perspective of functionality.
  • Void: one of the named natures or aspects of Origin.

These are descriptions, not definitions, and they are not exhaustive. Aspects may be independent, interact, influence one another, combine, separate and originate further aspects. The account does not treat Origin's aspects and what they originate as separate ultimate sources.

The related description of Zero — Idle State of Origin as Infinity before manifestation develops this aspect of the framework further.

Origin and imagination

Science of Origin uses imagination in a broad foundational sense. It is not limited to human fantasy, visualization or the production of mental images. It refers to the proposed capacity through which possibilities and manifestations arise from Origin.

Within this framework, manifestations are understood as imaginations of Origin. The imaginer and the imagined are not proposed as two independently existing ultimate sources; they are considered expressions associated with the same underlying Origin. The relationship between imagination and what comes into manifestation is explored further in Imagination and Manifestations.

The framework distinguishes spontaneous imagination, through which manifestations arise, from intentional imagination, which may operate within an existing manifestation. These distinctions and their mechanisms are developed further under Creation.

Origin, Consciousness and Freewill

Freewill is the freedom of change itself. Freewill and the freedom of change are the same thing, not two separate properties. This includes the freedom to change or not change, originate or not originate, manifest or not manifest, combine or separate, and transform or remain. Freewill is not a property that Origin or a manifestation possesses as something separate from itself.

All manifestations are understood as imaginations of Origin. Imagination and manifestation are not separate stages: an imagination is a manifestation, and the physical is also an imagination. Nothing exists independently outside Origin; anything imagined as existing outside Origin is also an imagination of Origin. See Consciousness & Freewill and the Core Principles of Origin.

Origin, idle and change

Zero is not a numerical starting point; it represents the idle state of Origin and Infinity before manifestation. Idle is not an absence of capability. Origin may remain idle, be active, or be idle in one aspect while active in another. Origination and cessation may occur simultaneously, spontaneously, deliberately or through mixed intentions.

The freedom of change includes the freedom not to change. Defining freewill would impose a limit, so “redefine itself” is not used as a description of this freedom. The guiding expression is: the only thing that does not change is change.

Original experiential account

The evolving first-person account What is The Ultimate Source records the initial experience of oneness on January 27, 2008 and subsequent clarifications through 2026. It is retained as a developing account, not a fixed historical statement.

Origin and origination

Origin and origination are related but distinct subjects. Origin concerns the fundamental source; origination concerns the capability and process through which manifestations come about and may give rise to further manifestations.

This distinction allows the research to examine both the source and the mechanisms or pathways through which things arise, without assuming that every particular manifestation is itself an ultimate origin.

See Origination for the related research subject.
