III · Structure · Source-based research account

Sample Human Body-Complex

A structured account of a twelve-body human body-complex, its four broad levels, the descriptions recorded in the original source, and a later interpretation in terms of conscious-loop structures.

Purpose and scope

This page organizes a particular twelve-body account for examination within Science of Origin. It is a sample body-complex described as an averaged structure; it is not presented as a universal template that every human necessarily shares in identical form.

The primary source is Multidimensional Body-Complex Structure (1): Single Complex, first edition 2010. The source records descriptions attributed to long-term practice, observation and experience. This page preserves that provenance while separating the original descriptions from later interpretations and research questions.

Read the original Awakenology source account.

Four broad levels and twelve bodies

The source organizes the sample into four broad levels: the physical body, spiritual bodies, the spiritual axis and the spiritual core.

A. Physical dimension

1. First Body — Physical Body. The body for physical experience in the material phenomenon world.

B. Spiritual dimension

The source groups bodies 2–10 within the spiritual dimension. It describes this group as relating to personality, emotions, thoughts and wisdom, and records different forms and functions for the individual bodies.

  1. Second Body — Blue Grid Light Body. Described as a consciousness-stream structure serving as a template for the physical body, organs and cells, and associated with physiological operation and sensory functions.
  2. Third Body — Rainbow Flowing Light Body. Described primarily in relation to personal emotions.
  3. Fourth Body — Yellow Grid Light Body. Described primarily in relation to personal thoughts, thought patterns and cognitive frameworks.
  4. Fifth Body — Rainbow Flowing Light Egg.
  5. Sixth Body — Blue Grid Light Egg.
  6. Seventh Body — Rainbow Radiant Egg.
  7. Eighth Body — Golden Grid Light Egg.
  8. Ninth Body — Transparent Radiant Egg.
  9. Tenth Body — Transparent Grid Light Egg.

The source places bodies 2–4 in the (quasi-)material phenomenon world and bodies 5–10 in six different spiritual worlds, each described as deeper and broader than the previous one. The names and descriptions are retained as the source's terminology.

C. Spiritual Axis dimension

11. Eleventh Body — Spiritual Axis. The source associates this level with individual intention, will, life plans and related functions.

D. Spiritual Core dimension

12. Twelfth Body — Spiritual Core. The source describes the Spiritual Core as formless and as the origin from which the spiritual axis, spiritual bodies and physical body are designed and created.

Relationships recorded in the source

The original account describes the physical body as existing within, and depending on, the spiritual bodies. It presents the spiritual axis and spiritual core as further levels involved in the formation and organization of the body-complex.

It also describes connections between corresponding chakras in different spiritual bodies, forming chakra-complexes through which streams of consciousness are said to circulate and interact. The account discusses independent survival of non-physical bodies, multiple body-complexes associated with one Spiritual Core, and other body-complex structures beyond the twelve-body sample.

These are descriptions and interpretations in the source account. This research page does not treat them as independently established biological or physical findings.

Later conscious-loop interpretation

A subsequent clarification within the Science of Origin framework describes conscious-loops as distributed throughout all twelve bodies of this sample body-complex. The loops are described as interconnected both directly and through connecting structures or channels.

In this interpretation, local loops and the higher-level consciousness framework both contribute to maintaining the overall body-complex. They coordinate and support one another. Individual loops may be replaced, redesigned or reorganized without necessarily dissolving the larger structure; the overall consciousness wireframe may also be redesigned while continuity of individual identity is preserved within the account.

This conscious-loop interpretation is a later extension of the source description. The 2010 source article does not itself set out this loop model. The distinction is retained here so that readers can trace which descriptions belong to the original account and which belong to subsequent framework development.

See Conscious-Loop Structures for the broader account of basic loops, interconnection, structural stability and transformation.

Observation and examination

The source lists practices through which its author reports observing the body-complex, including the Dimensional Switching Method (Shift Method), Inner Observation Method, Deep Meditation Method, Central Channel Chakra Method, Spiritual Eye Aura Method and Dream Observation Method. It describes observation over years of persistent practice.

The source also distinguishes direct personal observation from instrument-based observation and notes limits to the instruments it discusses. References to Kirlian photography and aura photography in the original article should not be read as independent confirmation of the full twelve-body model.

For examination, it is useful to keep separate: what was reported as directly perceived; the interpretation assigned to a perception; the terminology used to organize it; and any independent means of checking or comparing the account.

Questions for further research

  • What observations distinguish the twelve proposed bodies from one another?
  • How is the boundary between each body identified, and how does that boundary vary with the observer or method?
  • What is the relationship between the chakra-complexes described in the source and the later conscious-loop model?
  • How are local loops distributed within each body, and how are loops connected across bodies?
  • What observations would distinguish a local loop being replaced from a larger body-complex being reorganized?
  • How can continuity of the body-complex be examined through structural change?
  • What parts of the account can be independently observed, compared or reproduced, and what remains dependent on individual experience?
Research status: This page records a source-based experiential account and a later conscious-loop interpretation. It does not present the twelve-body structure, its functions or its proposed loop relationships as established findings of conventional biology or physics. The source, observer perspective, interpretations and unresolved questions remain open to examination and revision.
