What Is a Focal Point?

An Exploration of Souls and Individuality within One Consciousness

Initial research account · Research is ongoing

Abstract

A focal point is what humans call a soul, or a focal point within a soul that may comprise multiple focal points. A focal point is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

Focal points exist within the One Consciousness, which remains infinite, boundaryless and undivided. Individuality does not imply separation or division of Consciousness.

This paper presents the current understanding of focal points and souls through observation and experimentation, and identifies areas for continued investigation.

1. Introduction

A focal point is not merely a point from which something is observed. It is what humans call a soul, or a focal point within a soul that may comprise multiple focal points.

This understanding makes the focal point an important subject within Science of Consciousness. It provides a way to describe individual self-awareness and individuality within the One Consciousness, without treating these as separate or independent consciousnesses.

The account presented here reflects understanding developed through observation and experimentation. Further investigation may expand its details.

2. One Consciousness and Focal Points

There is only ONE Consciousness.

Consciousness is infinite and has no boundary at all. All souls, spirits, life forms and things exist within this One Consciousness.

Focal points exist within the One Consciousness without dividing or separating it. Their individuality does not imply that Consciousness is composed of independent consciousnesses.

The existence of individual focal points is therefore compatible with the Oneness of Consciousness.

3. Soul as a Focal Point or Combination of Focal Points

A soul, as called by humans, is a focal point or a combination of focal points within the One Consciousness.

A soul does not necessarily correspond to only one focal point. It may be represented by an individual focal point or by multiple focal points in combination.

The relationship between a soul and its focal point or focal points is an area of research informed by observation and experimentation.

4. Self-Awareness and Freewill

A focal point is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

Freewill is not an external property added to a focal point. It is its own nature and functionality.

Individual self-awareness exists within the One Freewill and does not imply the existence of multiple independent ultimate sources of Freewill.

Individual focal points can therefore express their own self-awareness while remaining within the One Consciousness and the One Freewill.

5. Individuality within Oneness

The existence of focal points provides a way to describe individuality within the One Consciousness.

Individuality and Oneness are not mutually exclusive. A focal point can have its own self-awareness and individual expression without being separate from Consciousness.

The One Consciousness remains undivided. The existence of individual souls and focal points does not create divisions within it.

6. Focal Points and Science of Consciousness

Focal points and souls are relevant research subjects within Science of Consciousness.

Through Spectrum / Spectra research, their characteristics, manifestations and relationships may be investigated.

The research approach emphasizes practical observation, measurable phenomena, quantifiable parameters, experimentation and reproducibility.

Further investigation may examine individual focal points, combinations of focal points, and their relationships within the One Consciousness.

7. Further Research

The nature and operation of focal points remain important subjects for continued investigation.

  • The characteristics of individual focal points.
  • The relationship between souls and individual or combined focal points.
  • Self-awareness as an expression of Freewill.
  • The manifestations and spectra associated with focal points.
  • The relationships among focal points within the One Consciousness.

These areas remain open to further observation, experimentation and discovery.

8. Summary

A focal point is what humans call a soul, or a constituent focal point within a soul that may comprise multiple focal points.

A focal point is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

All focal points exist within the One Consciousness, which remains infinite, boundaryless and undivided.

Individuality does not require separation. The existence of souls and focal points is an expression of individuality within the One Consciousness.

This is all that is being made available for now.
