What Is Consciousness?

An Exploration of the Nature and Unity of Consciousness

Initial research account · Research is ongoing

Abstract

This paper presents the current understanding of Consciousness developed through the research of Science of Origin.

Consciousness is boundaryless and infinite. There is only ONE Consciousness. It has no boundary at all and is never divided or separated.

The fundamental nature of life forms and things is the same: Consciousness. Their differences are associated with the Entropy of their conscious structures, rather than a difference in their fundamental nature.

This paper also distinguishes the true Consciousness of the soul from MIND, which is the conscious operating system of the human physical body. These are not the same.

Spectrum / Spectra provides the core methodology for further investigation into Consciousness and its relationships with life forms and things.

1. Introduction

What is Consciousness?

Consciousness is one of the fundamental subjects of Science of Origin. This research approaches the subject through observations, experiences and understandings developed through real-world investigation.

The central question is not merely how Consciousness operates, but what Consciousness fundamentally is.

The current understanding presented here is that there is only ONE Consciousness. It is boundaryless and infinite, without any boundary, division or separation.

Understanding this nature is essential to investigating the Nature of Life, the Nature of Things, Freewill and their relationships.

2. The Nature of Consciousness

Consciousness is boundaryless and infinite. It has no boundary at all.

There is no boundary within Consciousness, around Consciousness or separating one part of Consciousness from another. Consciousness is not divided into separate or independent units.

Boundaryless does not mean featureless. Infinite does not mean indistinguishable. Differences and distinct manifestations do not create boundaries in Consciousness.

Consciousness is not limited to what can currently be detected, recognized or understood. The limits of human observation and measurement should not be mistaken for limits of Consciousness itself.

This understanding provides the foundation for investigating Consciousness without imposing boundaries on its nature.

3. One Consciousness

There is only ONE Consciousness.

All spirits, souls, life forms and things exist within this One Consciousness. Nothing exists outside it, and nothing is separate from it.

One Consciousness does not mean that all manifestations must be identical. Differences, characteristics and diverse manifestations can exist without division or separation.

There is no boundary at all within this Oneness. Its infinite nature is not reduced or divided by the diversity of what exists within it.

This understanding differs from the idea that many independent consciousnesses collectively form a larger consciousness. There is only One Consciousness.

4. Consciousness in Life Forms and Things

The fundamental nature of life forms and things is the same: Consciousness.

The distinction between life forms and things does not arise from having fundamentally different kinds of Consciousness. The difference is associated with the Entropy of their conscious structures.

A form can be understood as a conscious structure or framework. Such a structure may be associated with a life form or a thing, and may exist in form or formlessness.

This perspective provides a basis for investigating Consciousness beyond the conventional distinction between living and non-living things.

Rather than assuming that Consciousness is exclusive to biological life, Science of Consciousness investigates the nature of both life forms and things through their conscious structures and manifestations.

The differences between them, including their characteristics and ways of manifesting, remain subjects for practical investigation.

5. Consciousness and Freewill

Consciousness and Freewill describe the same underlying nature from different perspectives.

  • Consciousness describes nature and attributes.
  • Freewill describes functionality and freedom inherent in that nature.

There is only ONE Consciousness and ONE Freewill. They should not be understood as two independent ultimate sources.

Freewill is the freedom of thought, imagination, creation and change. In nature, Freewill is Consciousness.

The relationship between Consciousness and Freewill is fundamental to understanding how the One expresses itself through diverse manifestations.

Further investigation into their attributes, functions and manifestations forms part of the Science of Consciousness and Science of Freewill.

6. Consciousness and MIND

Consciousness and MIND are distinct and should not be confused.

MIND is the conscious operating system of the human physical body. It is the consciousness of the human body, not the consciousness of the soul.

The soul's true Consciousness and the consciousness operating the human physical body are distinct aspects that must be understood separately.

MIND serves as the conscious operating system through which the human physical body functions. Its operation should not be mistaken for the full nature or extent of Consciousness.

This distinction is important when investigating the relationship among the soul, the human physical body, MIND and Consciousness.

The operation of MIND should not be used as a substitute for understanding the true Consciousness of the soul.

7. Consciousness, Awareness and Self-Awareness

Awareness and self-awareness are relevant to the investigation of Consciousness, but they should not be treated as interchangeable definitions of Consciousness.

Awareness does not necessarily require conscious recognition or understanding. The absence of an observable response or explicit recognition does not, by itself, establish the absence of awareness.

Self-awareness is also an important aspect of the investigation.

In the current understanding of Science of Origin, self-awareness is intrinsic to Freewill. Awareness and self-awareness should be investigated without assuming that they are limited to forms already recognized or understood.

The relationships among awareness, self-awareness, Consciousness and Freewill remain open for further investigation.

8. Consciousness and Spectrum / Spectra

Spectrum / Spectra is the core research methodology of Science of Consciousness.

It provides a way to investigate the characteristics, frequencies, compositions and relationships of research subjects through spectral investigation, measurement and analysis.

The Spectrum of Consciousness is intended to represent the complete spectrum of frequencies within the relevant domain, rather than merely a limited selection of detectable frequencies.

Spectra refers to multiple spectra or spectral representations used in comparative and related investigations.

One important research objective is to investigate whether:

Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness

This remains a research objective, not an established finding. It requires appropriate spectral investigation, measurement and evidence.

The Spectrum / Spectra methodology supports the broader investigation of Consciousness, including its relationship with life forms, things and their manifestations.

9. Further Research

The understanding presented in this paper provides a foundation for further research. It does not imply that every aspect of Consciousness has already been investigated or established.

  • The nature and characteristics of Consciousness as boundaryless and infinite.
  • The relationship between the true Consciousness of the soul and the MIND of the human physical body.
  • The nature and characteristics of conscious structures in life forms and things.
  • The relationship between Entropy and conscious structures.
  • The relationships among Consciousness, Freewill, awareness and self-awareness.
  • The complete Spectrum of Consciousness and its relationship with the Spectrum of Things.
  • The development of appropriate methods for spectral investigation, measurement and analysis.

These research areas should remain open to new observations, experiences and discoveries.

Science of Consciousness aims to develop practical, measurable, experimental and reproducible approaches to investigating these subjects, while remaining open to possibilities not yet understood.

10. Summary

Consciousness is boundaryless and infinite. There is only ONE Consciousness, and it has no boundary at all.

All spirits, souls, life forms and things exist within this One Consciousness. Nothing is outside it or separate from it.

The fundamental nature of life forms and things is the same: Consciousness. Their differences are associated with the Entropy of their conscious structures.

Consciousness and Freewill describe the same underlying nature from different perspectives: Consciousness describes nature and attributes, while Freewill describes functionality and freedom.

MIND is the conscious operating system of the human physical body. It is the consciousness of the human body, not the consciousness of the soul. These must be understood as distinct aspects.

Spectrum / Spectra provides the core methodology for investigating Consciousness and its relationships with life forms, things and their manifestations.

This understanding provides a foundation for continued research without assuming that the infinite nature of Consciousness can be fully represented by current knowledge or methods.

Research Status

This paper presents the current understanding of Consciousness within Science of Origin. The statements about One Consciousness, its boundaryless and infinite nature, and the distinction between the soul's true Consciousness and MIND reflect the current research understanding presented here.

Other areas identified for further research remain open for investigation. In particular, the proposed equivalence between the Spectrum of Things and the Spectrum of Consciousness remains a research objective.

The paper may be expanded and refined as further observations, experiences and discoveries become available.

This is all that is being made available for now.
