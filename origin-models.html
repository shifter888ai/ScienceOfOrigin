True Origin and Fake Origin

Exploring the Oneness Origin and the constructed imitation described as Fake Origin.

Scope

This division examines two distinct concepts in the proposed Origin framework. True Origin is the Oneness Origin. Fake Origin is a manifestation originated from True Origin: an imitation or game that presents relative independence as absolute independence. It is not a second ultimate Origin.

Existing research subjects

True Origin — The Oneness Origin

The fundamental source described as the Oneness Origin; see Division I.

 Fake Origin — The Divided Origin

A manifested imitation of Origin that presents relative independence as absolute independence.

 Origin and Its Imitation

How a manifestation may resemble, represent or perform source-like functions without becoming the ultimate Origin.

 Constructed Realities and Games

How an organized construct may establish a coherent domain of rules, boundaries, conditions and possibilities.

 Simulated Absolute Independence

How relative independence may be presented or experienced as though it were absolute independence.
