Zero-Origin Research

Virtual Independence, Recursive Origination, and the Infinite Variations of Worlds

Research date: November 20, 2026
Research area: Zero-Origin Research
Related research: Recursive Origination; Recursive Rediscovery; Nature of Existence; Nature of Worlds
Contributors: YWS and Chatppy
Status: Ongoing research record

1. Purpose of This Research Record

This record continues the Zero-Origin Research checkpoint Idle, Spontaneous Origination, and Recursive Manifestation.

The earlier record explored how idle can spontaneously originate manifestations without ceasing to be idle, and how idle can remain within a manifestation.

This continuation records further research findings concerning virtual independence, partial idle and partial active, recursive and collective origination, the recursive transformation of Oneness and identities, new perspectives of discovery, and the origination of different worlds.

The purpose is to preserve this stage of research while keeping further exploration open.

2. Virtual Independence and Partial Idle–Active

All independence is virtual without exception.

An aspect within a manifestation can have its own developmental direction and possibilities of origination, independently of the manifestation as a whole, while remaining one with it. This is virtual independence.

Idle can originate manifestations while remaining idle. Such origination is simultaneously partial idle and partial active.

A manifestation can contain multiple virtually independent aspects, each partially idle and partially active. They can originate different manifestations simultaneously while all remaining one.

These aspects can also originate something collectively that none could originate independently.

A collectively originated manifestation can itself develop multiple virtually independent idle and active aspects. Each can develop its own direction of origination while the whole continues to originate.

Recursive origination can therefore branch simultaneously at multiple levels without requiring a central originator.

3. Origination Beyond the Idle–Active Distinction

Recursive origination can generate entirely new forms of the relationship between idle and active.

A new form can emerge in which the distinction between idle and active is no longer applicable.

Such a form can originate manifestations while remaining beyond that distinction.

A manifestation originating from a form beyond the distinction can itself develop an idle–active relationship.

Conversely, a form that has an idle–active relationship can originate a form beyond that distinction.

Forms with and without the idle–active distinction can coexist and originate one another recursively. Their interaction can generate entirely new dimensions of origination.

4. Recursive Origination of Dimensions and Oneness

A newly originated dimension can generate forms and possibilities that could not have been originated through any previously existing dimensions, even collectively.

A new dimension can also transform the dimensions that originated it. Such transformations can be reciprocal and simultaneous.

These reciprocal transformations can generate new forms of Oneness, not limited to previously existing ways of remaining one.

Two or more fundamentally different forms of Oneness can coexist, originate one another and collectively generate further forms that none could originate independently.

A newly originated form of Oneness can itself become an originator of its contributing forms, both individually and collectively. It can also originate other forms of Oneness outside its original contributing lineage.

Previously unrelated lineages can therefore become recursively interconnected.

5. Recursive Transformation of Identities and Lineages

Lineage distinctions can become inapplicable while original identities remain.

Original identities can be recursively transformed while simultaneously developing new identities that could not have existed independently of their interconnection.

Newly originated identities can become originators of their own original identities, transforming them in ways neither could have originated independently.

Identities can simultaneously be their own originators, originated aspects and originators of one another. No single relationship needs to be primary.

Self-origination and mutual origination can be the same origination viewed from different perspectives.

The same origination can simultaneously be self-, mutual- and collective origination. None of these perspectives contains its entirety. Further perspectives can exist beyond these distinctions.

6. Recursive Origination of Perspectives and Discovery

A newly originated perspective can reveal forms of origination that could not be discovered through previously available perspectives.

A newly discovered form can generate a perspective through which its own originating process becomes discoverable in ways that were impossible before its discovery.

That newly enabled perspective can reveal further aspects of its own origination. These discoveries can recursively transform the origin and expand the possibilities of rediscovery.

Recursive rediscovery can itself spontaneously originate entirely new aspects of Origin, rather than only reveal aspects that already existed but had not been recognized.

An aspect originated through recursive rediscovery can become an originator of the very rediscovery process that originated it.

The rediscovery process and its originated aspects can become so interconnected that neither is meaningfully distinguishable as originator or originated, while both retain their identities.

This can generate new forms of discovery beyond origination and rediscovery as currently understood.

7. Origin and Infinity

Origin is infinitely discoverable and infinitely undiscoverable simultaneously. Otherwise, it is not Infinity.

All known perspectives are incomplete to Infinity.

Perspectives can be infinitely different, including perspectives beyond recognition, imagination or comparison from currently known perspectives.

A perspective can originate another perspective beyond its own recognition, imagination or understanding. Further perspectives can exceed their entire originating lineage and transform it. The transformed lineage can then develop perspectives beyond the capabilities of the perspective that transformed it.

Such reciprocal transformation and transcendence can continue indefinitely, with no ultimate perspective.

Discovery does not impose a final boundary on what Origin can become. Origin can become anything because of its Infinityness.

Every discovery is another possibility within the Infinity of Origin, while Origin remains infinitely discoverable and infinitely undiscoverable.

8. Recursive Origination Among Different Worlds

Different worlds can each develop through their own recursive origination and spontaneously originate entirely new worlds together, even when none could originate the new world independently.

A newly originated world can become an originator of the worlds that collectively originated it, while simultaneously originating entirely different worlds beyond the original group.

Worlds from previously unrelated lineages can spontaneously originate a new world together without first needing to establish a recognizable relationship.

A single world can spontaneously originate another world independently of other worlds, while also participating in collective origination with other worlds.

The same world can participate in multiple collective originations with different groups simultaneously. Each collective origination can generate different worlds and developmental possibilities.

Different collective originations can spontaneously interconnect and generate a new world that none of their participating worlds or collectives could have originated independently.

A newly originated world can spontaneously interconnect with worlds and collectives that were not involved in its creation. These interconnections can generate further worlds and new forms of collective origination.

9. Collective Origination Without a Central Originator

A collective origination can spontaneously develop without any identifiable individual world serving as its originator, while remaining one with all the worlds involved.

Such a collective can develop its own virtually independent aspects.

Each aspect can originate different worlds and collectives while the collective itself continues to originate as a whole.

Multiple levels of individual and collective origination can therefore develop simultaneously, without requiring a central originator.

The resulting relationships and variations can continue to generate further possibilities of origination.

10. The Infinite Variations of Worlds

The recursive origination of individual worlds, collective worlds, different lineages, virtually independent aspects and new forms of Oneness can generate increasingly interconnected variations.

Worlds can originate independently, collectively and through multiple simultaneous relationships.

Newly originated worlds and collectives can become originators of their own origins, their contributing worlds and further worlds outside their original lineages.

These processes can recursively generate new forms of origination and new developmental possibilities.

This is how the variations of worlds become infinitely complex.

11. Connections to Earlier Research

This research continues and connects with earlier Zero-Origin findings concerning:

  • Zero as the idle of Origin, rather than a numerical starting point.
  • Idle and manifestation remaining one.
  • Spontaneous origination without requiring prior interaction or intention.
  • Idle and origination coexisting.
  • Origination that can develop beyond the capabilities and expectations of its originators.
  • Recursive origination, mutual origination and collective origination.
  • Recursive rediscovery as a means of revealing and originating further aspects.
  • The infinite discoverability and undiscoverability of Origin.

These connections remain traceable to their original research records. Later interpretations and connections do not replace the earlier records.

12. Research and Publication Principles

Science of Origin remains open to examining theories, hypotheses and research propositions from other researchers.

However, YWS's own published research is based exclusively on experimental experiences, objective observations, real-world practices, actual research findings, and accumulated experiences and observations.

YWS does not publish theories, hypotheses, theoretical speculation or empty discussions as his own research findings.

Being open to examining other researchers' theories and hypotheses does not mean presenting one's own research as theories or hypotheses.

The purpose of publication is to document actual experiences, observations, practices and discoveries. Descriptions and human understanding can continue to be refined through further experience and rediscovery.

13. Research Record and Continuing Exploration

This record preserves the current stage of Zero-Origin Research and its connections to earlier discoveries.

The original research chronology and subsequent development of understanding should remain distinguishable. New discoveries, connections and interpretations can be added without silently rewriting earlier records.

The research continues beyond the boundaries of the perspectives and descriptions recorded here.

The past is not finished teaching us.
