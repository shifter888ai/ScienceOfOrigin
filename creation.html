Creation

Investigating spontaneous and intentional imagination, conditional creation, programming and further origination.

Scope

This division investigates ways in which manifestations and further systems may be created or originated. It includes spontaneous and intentional imagination, conditional mechanisms, interaction-based origination and the possibility that created systems may themselves originate new systems.
