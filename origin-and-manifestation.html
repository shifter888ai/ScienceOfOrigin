Origin and Manifestation

Research Observations on Origin and Unlimited Manifestation Levels

This paper shares current research observations concerning Origin, manifestation levels, and the relationships and characteristics observed among those levels.

1. Origin and Manifestation

The research distinguishes Origin without manifestation from the levels of manifestation.

  • 0 — Origin: The source without manifestation.
  • 1 — First level: The first level of manifestation.
  • 2 — Second level: The second level of manifestation.

Levels 3, 4 and onward continue without an upper limit. Manifestation levels are therefore unlimited.

2. Simultaneous Existence and Operation

All levels exist and operate simultaneously.

3. Overlap

All levels overlap. Overlap is not the same as mixing, and overlapping levels do not necessarily mix.

4. Distinguishability

Levels are not always distinguishable. Some may be distinguishable in a given circumstance, while others may not be. Boundaries or individual identities may not always be identifiable.

Indistinguishability does not, by itself, mean that a level has ceased to exist or operate.

5. Mixing

Any levels may mix. Mixing is possible, but it is not necessary for all overlapping levels to mix.

Mixing may occur at any time and may occur without order. In some instances of mixing, the boundaries or individual identities of the levels involved may not be distinguishable, while their existence and operation continue.

6. Overlap and Mixing Are Different

The distinction is direct:

  • All levels overlap.
  • Any levels may mix.
  • Overlap does not necessarily involve mixing.
  • Mixing may occur without order.
  • Levels involved in mixing are not necessarily distinguishable.

Universal overlap must not be interpreted as universal mixing.

7. Ongoing Research

These observations are shared as part of ongoing research into Origin and manifestation. Further observation and experimentation may reveal additional characteristics, relationships and discoveries.

The investigation remains open. The descriptions shared here are not a final or fixed account; research and understanding continue to develop.
