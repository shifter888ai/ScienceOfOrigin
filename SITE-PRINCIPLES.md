# Site Principles

These principles govern the research content and communication of the Science of Origin website. They are the master editorial principles for every page and publication on the site.

They express the research approach behind the work and the way its findings are presented to readers.

## 1. Practical Research

Research on this site is grounded in real-world observations, practices, experiments, and experiences.

- Present what has actually been observed, practised, experienced, or established through research.
- State the basis of each finding clearly so readers can understand where it comes from.
- Keep findings distinct from questions, ongoing investigations, and matters that have not yet been established.
- Describe research methods and results clearly, including measurement and reproducibility where applicable.
- Remain open to further observation, deeper understanding, and new discoveries.
- Preserve the original context, chronology, and source of research records. Later explanations or clarifications must not be presented as if they were part of the original record.

The content should reflect the research as it is actually conducted and the findings as they are actually reached.

## 2. Humble and Objective Communication

Present discoveries with humility and objectivity. The purpose is to communicate what has been found clearly and honestly, while allowing readers the freedom to examine and understand it for themselves.

- **Be humble.** Present discoveries calmly and respectfully. Do not demand agreement, judge readers, or belittle different understandings.
- **Be objective.** State the facts of findings honestly and objectively. Add no positive or negative slant. Present what was found as it is, without exaggeration, understatement, or distortion.
- Explain unfamiliar concepts clearly and do not assume that readers already share the same understanding.
- Be firm and precise about the findings, while remaining considerate toward the reader.
- Do not weaken, conceal, or alter a finding merely to make it more comfortable or acceptable to readers.
- Make clear what a statement is based on and do not present an interpretation, suggestion, or unresolved question as an established finding.

Humility governs the manner of communication; objectivity governs the faithful presentation of findings.

## Application

These principles apply to the entire website, including its homepage, About page, research framework, all research divisions, individual research pages, records, references, and future publications.

When reviewing or revising any page:

1. Check that its content reflects the practical research described in Principle 1.
2. Check that its wording follows the humble and objective communication described in Principle 2.
3. Preserve the meaning and provenance of original research records.
4. Make editorial changes only where they improve accuracy, clarity, or consistency with these principles.

These principles are the primary editorial reference for the Science of Origin website. Site design and technical conventions are maintained separately in `SITE-STANDARDS.md`.
