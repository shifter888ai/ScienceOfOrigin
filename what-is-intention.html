What Is Intention?

An Exploration of Intention and Its Expressions

Public research exploration · Research is ongoing

Abstract

This paper explores the nature of intention within the developing research framework of Science of Origin.

It considers the relationship between intention, Origin, Freewill, origination and manifestation, and examines spontaneous intention, intentional intention and cases in which spontaneous and intentional aspects occur together.

The paper also records an observation concerning the approximate proportions of spontaneous and intentional intention. These proportions are averages that fluctuate; they are not fixed values or established population statistics.

This is an initial research account. The descriptions and observations provide a basis for continued exploration, while their scope, relationships, measurement and reproducibility remain open for further development.

1. Introduction

What is intention?

Intention is an important aspect of the exploration of Origin and origination. It is relevant to how manifestations arise, how origination is directed, and how different expressions of Freewill may be observed.

This paper approaches intention through the current Science of Origin framework. It does not assume that familiar everyday meanings fully describe the nature or range of intention being investigated.

The purpose at this stage is to record the distinctions and observations currently available, while leaving room for further clarification and discovery.

2. Intention and Origin

There is only ONE Freewill, which is Origin.

Intention is explored in relation to this One Freewill. It is not treated as an independent ultimate source, nor does the appearance of different intentions imply that the One Freewill has been divided into separate ultimate sources.

All existences are imaginations within the only One Freewill. The infinite originating capability is intrinsic to Freewill.

Within this framework, intention is involved in directing origination. The relationship between intention and the manifestation that follows is not assumed to be automatic or one-to-one.

3. Intention and Origination

Intention and origination are related, but they are not interchangeable terms.

Intention directs origination. Origination concerns the coming-about of manifestations. An intention may therefore be present without the intended manifestation necessarily being actualized.

The presence of an intention does not, by itself, establish that the intended result will occur. An intended origination may or may not be actualized, and the circumstances and relationships involved remain subjects for investigation.

Manifestations may appear finite without limiting the infinite originating capability of Origin.

4. Spontaneous Intention

Spontaneous intention is intention that arises spontaneously.

It can arise randomly. It should not automatically be interpreted as a concealed deliberate plan, as something that must have been intentionally prepared in advance, or as something determined by prior experience.

Spontaneous intention is already intention when it arises. It does not first exist as something other than intention and then become intention at a later stage. Its spontaneous emergence is not a separate preliminary stage that must be completed before it qualifies as intention.

The ways in which spontaneous intention arises, including the range of circumstances in which it may occur, remain open for further exploration.

5. Intentional Intention

Intentional intention has deliberate direction.

Its distinguishing characteristic in this exploration is its deliberate direction or purpose. This distinguishes it from spontaneous intention without making it a separate ultimate source or a different fundamental nature from intention itself.

Intentional intention is also related to the One Freewill and to the originating capability intrinsic to Freewill.

The relationship between deliberate direction, experience and the actualization of an intended manifestation requires further examination. No assumption is made here that deliberate direction guarantees a particular outcome.

6. Spontaneous and Intentional Aspects Together

Spontaneous and intentional aspects are not always observed as mutually exclusive categories.

Mixed cases are common. A particular case may involve spontaneous and intentional aspects together, rather than fitting exclusively into one category.

The distinction between spontaneous and intentional intention therefore does not require every instance of intention to be classified as purely one or the other.

How these aspects combine, interact or vary within a particular instance remains an area for continued observation and clarification.

7. An Observation of Approximate Proportions

An observation recorded in this research has described the average proportion as approximately:

  • 98% spontaneous intention
  • 2% intentional intention

These figures describe approximate averages from the observation, not fixed proportions. They fluctuate, and mixed cases are common.

They are not presented as population statistics or as independently established measurements. The scope of the observation, its measurement basis and the procedures required for reproducibility have not been made available in this paper.

The figures should therefore be read as a record of the current observation, not as universal values or a predetermined ratio.

8. Intention, Experience and Manifestation

Experience differentiates expressions of origination. This is an important part of the current exploration of how intention and origination are expressed.

This does not mean that spontaneous intention must be caused or determined by prior experience. Spontaneous intention can arise randomly, and its emergence should not automatically be explained as the result of an earlier deliberate process.

Likewise, the presence of deliberate intention does not establish that every aspect of a resulting manifestation was anticipated, specified or controlled.

The relationships among experience, intention, origination and manifestation require further investigation. They should not be collapsed into a single process or treated as interchangeable descriptions.

9. Further Research

The following areas remain open for continued exploration:

  • How spontaneous intention arises, including the circumstances in which it may arise randomly.
  • How intentional intention develops or expresses deliberate direction.
  • How spontaneous and intentional aspects combine in common mixed cases.
  • How experience differentiates expressions of origination without necessarily determining spontaneous intention.
  • How intention directs origination in different circumstances.
  • Why an intended origination may or may not be actualized.
  • How the approximate observed proportions fluctuate and how the observation may be documented and examined further.
  • Which aspects of intention can be investigated through practical observation, measurement and reproducible methods.

These are directions for further research, not claims that the mechanisms or answers have already been established.

Summary

This initial account explores intention in relation to the One Freewill, Origin and origination. It distinguishes spontaneous intention, which arises spontaneously and can be random, from intentional intention, which has deliberate direction.

Spontaneous intention is already intention when it arises. Spontaneous and intentional aspects can also occur together, and mixed cases are common. The approximate 98% / 2% proportions recorded here are fluctuating averages from an observation, not fixed values or population statistics.

The relationships among intention, experience, origination and actualization remain open to further exploration. Research is ongoing. The exploration of Origin has no final conclusion.

Research status: This is an initial public research account of current propositions and observations. It is intended to develop as the research continues; it does not present every relationship or mechanism as independently established.

This is all that is being made available for now.
