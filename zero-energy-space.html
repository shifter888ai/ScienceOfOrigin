III · Structure · Source-based research account

Zero-Energy Space

A structured account of the proposed self-cycling spatial structure, the development described in its original source, and the later distinction between a Zero-Energy Space and its underlying conscious-loop.

Purpose and source

This page organizes the account presented in What is Zero-Energy Space, dated 2019-11-16, for examination within Science of Origin. The original article describes a spatial structure intended to address the natural dissipation of ordinary space, followed by an account of experimentation and expansion into multiple spaces.

Read the original Awakenology source account.

The descriptions below are attributed to that source. They are not presented as independently verified descriptions of physical space, energy conservation or cosmological history.

A. Ordinary space — the source account

The source describes ordinary spaces as individual bubble-like spaces created by one or more spirits (souls) through imagination. It states that each space requires attention, continuous re-creation and ongoing energy input to persist. Without continued attention, a space is described as naturally dissipating over time.

The source expresses the relationship as “Attention = creation of energy” and attributes the natural dissipation of space to non-conservation of energy. It extends the same account to objects, describing them as composed of spatial structures that also require continuous attention to avoid collapse and dissipation.

B. Zero-Energy Space — proposed characteristics

The source says that a special spatial structure called “Zero-Energy Space” was invented to address the natural dissipation described above. Its stated characteristics are:

  1. Cyclic structure. Energy entering the space is described as moving through a self-cycling or closed-loop structure, with permanent or near-permanent circulation and little or no leakage.
  2. Zero energy. The initial energy used to create the space is described as cycling permanently or near-permanently. The space therefore does not naturally dissipate and is said to require zero or nearly zero additional energy consumption.
  3. New energy. Additional energy entering the structure is described as automatically creating new space, allowing the space to expand without dissipating.
  4. Objects. Objects made of Zero-Energy Space are described as able to persist without additional energy input.
  5. Multi-space cyclic conversion. Multiple spaces may be created within a Zero-Energy Space. If one of these spaces disappears, its energy is described as continuing to cycle among the other spaces rather than returning to zero.

These characteristics are the claims and terminology of the original account. “Zero energy” in the title refers to the claimed absence, or near absence, of ongoing energy consumption by the space; it does not mean that the initial energy input is described as nonexistent.

C. Experimentation and development — as reported

The original article reports that, after the technology was invented, an experimental team of eight spirits was formed to conduct numerous experiments intended to verify and improve the practical application of the Zero-Energy Space patent. The team was named “Zero,” and the team leader was also called “Zero.”

Following what the source describes as successful small-scale trials, the Zero-Energy Space was provided as a template to other creators. They are described as creating many small spaces and subsequently merging them into medium-sized spaces.

The account then describes further creators joining, creating additional spaces and expanding the system through mergers and acquisitions. It says that large, medium and many small Zero-Energy Spaces eventually coexisted, and that Zero-Energy Space became known as the “Material World,” also called the “Physical Phenomenal World.”

This is the development sequence reported by the source. The present page records it as part of the account rather than asserting it as an independently established history of the physical universe.

D. Energy circulation and conversion — source terminology

The source calls the cyclic structure of Zero-Energy Space “energy conservation” or “near conservation.” It calls the circulation and conversion among multiple spaces “energy conversion,” and describes this as the principle and process through which the material universe is created.

These expressions are used here in the sense assigned by the source account. Their relationship to established physical definitions and measurements of energy conservation, energy transfer and space remains an open question, not an assumed equivalence.

E. Later conscious-loop interpretation

A subsequent clarification within the Science of Origin framework distinguishes the Zero-Energy Space from the structure underlying it: the conscious-loop is the basic structure that makes a Zero-Energy Space possible; the space itself is not the conscious-loop.

Within this later interpretation, each Zero-Energy Space requires its own underlying conscious-loop. The loop provides the basic closed-loop structure, while the Zero-Energy Space is the spatial structure maintained through it. This distinction is important: the terms “conscious-loop” and “Zero-Energy Space” are related, but they are not interchangeable.

The broader framework describes the Zero-Energy design as the original basic loop-structure, general to the physical universe. That is a later framework interpretation and should be read separately from the wording of the 2019 source article.

See the consolidated Conscious-Loop Model for the wider evolving research framework.

Questions for further research

  • What is the operational meaning of a “space” in this account, and how is its boundary identified?
  • What observations distinguish a self-cycling space from a space that requires continuing external input?
  • How is energy entering, circulating within and leaving a proposed Zero-Energy Space identified or quantified?
  • What would count as evidence of leakage, near-conservation or expansion through additional energy?
  • How are multiple spaces connected, and what determines the circulation of energy when one space disappears?
  • What observations distinguish the underlying conscious-loop from the spatial structure it is proposed to maintain?
  • How could the reported small-scale experiments and later expansion sequence be examined or independently reproduced?
  • What is the relationship, if any, between the source's use of “energy conservation” and established physical definitions?
