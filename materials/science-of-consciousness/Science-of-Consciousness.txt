# Science of Consciousness
## Project Description & Research Framework

### 1. Overview

Science of Consciousness is a new science research project dedicated to investigating the fundamental nature of Life and Things through the scientific study of Consciousness, Freewill, and Spectrum.

The project aims to develop a unified scientific framework for understanding the Nature of Life and the Nature of Things, exploring their fundamental properties, relationships, and underlying principles.

**Spectrum / Spectra serves as the core research methodology of Science of Consciousness.** Through spectral investigation, measurement, and analysis, the project seeks to identify, examine, and establish the fundamental properties, frequencies, structures, and relationships of its research subjects.

The project emphasizes practical investigation, measurable phenomena, quantifiable parameters, experimentation, and reproducible results.

### 2. Research Scope

The research encompasses two fundamental domains:

- **Nature of Life** — Investigation into the fundamental nature, properties, and phenomena of life.
- **Nature of Things** — Investigation into the fundamental nature, properties, and phenomena of things, including their possible relationship with consciousness.

### 3. Core Research Fields

The project is founded on four core research fields:

1. **Science of Consciousness** — Research into the fundamental nature, properties, and mechanisms of consciousness.

2. **Science of Freewill** — Research into the fundamental nature, properties, and mechanisms of freewill.

3. **Spectrum of Consciousness** — Research into the complete spectrum of frequencies of consciousness, encompassing all possible frequencies within the domain being investigated.

4. **Spectrum of Things** — Research into the complete spectrum of frequencies of things, encompassing all possible frequencies within the domain being investigated.

### 4. Core Research Methodology: Spectrum / Spectra

Spectrum / Spectra is the central methodology of Science of Consciousness.

It provides the framework for investigating and analyzing the complete range of frequencies and their properties within the domains of consciousness and things.

The methodology aims to:

- Identify and investigate the full spectrum of the research subjects.
- Develop appropriate methods for spectral measurement and analysis.
- Examine relationships, similarities, differences, and correspondences between spectra.
- Establish measurable and quantifiable parameters.
- Validate findings through systematic experimentation and reproducibility.

Spectrum is intended to represent the entirety of frequencies within the relevant research domain, rather than merely a limited selection or range.

Spectra refers to multiple spectra or spectral representations used in comparative and related investigations.

#### Primary Research Objective: Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness

A central objective is to investigate and establish, through Spectrum / Spectra research, whether:

**Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness**

This research seeks to determine whether the complete spectrum of things and the complete spectrum of consciousness are fundamentally equivalent.

This equivalence remains a research objective, not an established scientific conclusion. It must be examined through appropriate spectral definitions, measurement methods, experimentation, and reproducible evidence.

### 5. Applications

Science of Consciousness encompasses five application research fields:

1. **Freedomality of Consciousness** — Research into freedomality as a property of consciousness and its possible measurement and quantification.

2. **Entropy of Consciousness** — Research into entropy in relation to consciousness, including its properties, characteristics, and measurable aspects.

3. **Strength of Consciousness of Things** — Research into the strength of consciousness in relation to things, including its properties and potential measurement.

4. **Awakenology** — Applied research into methods of awakening humanity and the development of consciousness.

5. **Exincarnology** — Applied research into methods of exincarnation (脱離輪迴), focusing on liberation from the reincarnation cycle.

### 6. Scientific Research Principles

The project follows these fundamental principles:

- **Spectrum-Based Research:** Use Spectrum / Spectra as the central methodology for investigating research subjects and their relationships.
- **Practical Research:** Prioritize real-world practices, observations, and experiences.
- **Measurability:** Develop methods to measure and quantify research subjects and their properties.
- **Experimentation:** Establish research findings through systematic experiments.
- **Reproducibility:** Develop methods and procedures that allow results to be independently reproduced.
- **Evidence-Based Conclusions:** Distinguish established findings from research objectives and propositions still under investigation.
- **Open Research:** Remain open to new possibilities and discoveries without prematurely restricting research directions.
- **Expandability:** Allow additional research fields, applications, and discoveries to develop within the overall framework.

### 7. Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision of Science of Consciousness is to establish a comprehensive and unified scientific understanding of the Nature of Life and the Nature of Things.

By making Spectrum / Spectra the central research methodology, the project seeks to investigate fundamental relationships among consciousness, freewill, life, and things.

Its ultimate aim is to develop a practical, measurable, experimental, and reproducible science capable of investigating phenomena that remain insufficiently understood and potentially discovering new fundamental relationships.
