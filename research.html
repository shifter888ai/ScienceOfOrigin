Research as one continuous undertaking

This section provides a human-readable entry point to research subjects, current perspectives and historical records across Science of Origin. These are organizational views for navigation, not separate bodies of research. Subjects and records may overlap completely and may be relevant across the entire undertaking.

Research may include observation, experience, measurement, experimentation, dialogue, interpretation and other approaches as appropriate to the subject. The basis and status of material should remain clear.
