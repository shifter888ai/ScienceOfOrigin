Read the Research Framework

How to read the network

  • Major divisions organize broad research domains.
  • Fundamental topics identify central subjects within those domains.
  • Extended topics provide more specific directions for investigation.
  • Connections show relationships across the map; a connection is not, by itself, a claim of proof or experimental confirmation.

The initial connections are editorial links based on the current framework and topic pages. They indicate that subjects are related for research purposes; they do not automatically assert causation, dependency or an experimentally established relationship. Relationship types and research status can be made more precise as the map develops.
