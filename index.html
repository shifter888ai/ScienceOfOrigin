Science of Origin

An evolving research project exploring Origin, origination, manifestations, and the relationships among them.

What is Science of Origin?

Science of Origin is an open research project concerned with the fundamental source of existence, the processes through which things originate, their manifestations, and the relationships connecting them.

The meaning and scope of Origin remain subjects of exploration. The project does not begin by assuming that every question already has an established answer.

Our starting points

Origin

The fundamental source and the central subject of investigation.

 Origination

The process through which manifestations originate.

 Research Framework

The scope, questions and principles guiding the work.

An evolving framework

The project is being developed through discussion, research records and progressively refined web pages. Concepts and models may change as the work develops.

We distinguish ideas under consideration from observations, evidence and conclusions that have been sufficiently established.
