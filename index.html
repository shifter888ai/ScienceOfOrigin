What is Science of Origin?

Science of Origin is an open research project concerned with Origin, Origination, the fundamental nature of existence, and how everything that exists may be understood.

The project explores foundational concepts and possible relationships through observation, experience and experimentation. Its foundational description places Infinity first as the closest definitional description of Origin, followed by Spirit, Consciousness, Freewill and Voidness as different perspectives on the same Ultimate Source.

Read the Core Principles of Origin for a fuller introduction to the framework and its experimental research approach.

The seven divisions

The framework is organized into seven connected divisions. Each division provides a primary home for its subjects while allowing cross-references where topics overlap.

I. Foundations — Origin and Origination

Origin, Consciousness / Freewill, imagination, origination and manifestation.

 II. Nature of Existence

The nature and forms of existence: things, life, energy, matter, information, space and time.

 III. Structure — Oneness, Division and Boundaries

Systems, rules, organization, relationships, boundaries, multiplicity and independence.

 IV. Origin Models — True Origin and Fake Origin

The Oneness Origin and the constructed imitation described as Fake Origin.

 V. Creation — Creation and Origination Mechanisms

Spontaneous and intentional imagination, conditional creation and further origination.

 VI. Dynamics — Operation and Development

Interactions, causality, states, processes, transformation, emergence and possibility.

 VII. Research — Investigation and Verification

Observation, measurement, knowledge, uncertainty, research methods and findings.

An evolving framework

The seven divisions provide an expandable structure, not limits on what Science of Origin may investigate. Subjects may be refined, added or reorganized as the work develops.

Existing research pages are being retained and connected to the new structure. The framework distinguishes ideas under consideration from observations, evidence and conclusions that have been sufficiently established.

Working status: This is an evolving research framework. Its content and structure may change as the research develops.
