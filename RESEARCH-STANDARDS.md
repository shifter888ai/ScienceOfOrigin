# Research Standards

These are practical working principles for presenting and developing Science of Origin research. They remain open to refinement.

## Starting points

Research may begin with an observation, an experience, a question or an existing understanding. A first-hand observation remains a legitimate starting point even when it has not been independently reproduced.

## Keep distinctions clear

When relevant, distinguish:

- **Observation or experience:** what was directly observed or experienced, with context where available.
- **Interpretation or explanation:** how an observation or experience is understood.
- **Experimental examination:** what was examined through a described procedure.
- **Finding:** what the examination supports, within its stated scope and conditions.
- **Independent reproduction:** what has been reproduced independently.
- **Open investigation:** what remains uncertain, unexplained or to be examined.

Do not collapse these into one status. Do not dismiss an observation merely because it is not independently reproduced; equally, do not describe it as an independently established result when it is not.

## Communicate responsibly

- Present the basis and limits of important statements as clearly as practical.
- Distinguish research objectives and proposed relationships from established findings.
- Do not infer a cause, intention or absence solely from a lack of observation.
- Keep unfamiliar possibilities and non-manifestation open to investigation.
- Share research for reference, not instruction; readers remain free to examine it and reach their own conclusions.

## Records

As research develops, preserve useful context: the question, subject, method or procedure, observations, evidence, interpretation, current status and remaining questions. Use only the detail appropriate to the work; do not force every inquiry into an elaborate template.

## Evolution

These standards guide current practice; they do not define the limits of research. Revise them as methods, experience and understanding develop. No current method, record or conclusion is assumed to exhaust Origin or its manifestations.
