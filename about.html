About Science of Origin

An open, evolving research project exploring Origin, Origination, and the fundamental nature of everything that exists.

Founder

Science of Origin is founded by YWS.

Scope of Research

Science of Origin investigates questions concerning Origin and Origination, the nature of existence, and the structures, relationships and dynamics through which things may exist and develop.

Its scope is intentionally broad. The project brings foundational questions together with investigations of manifestations, systems, boundaries, creation, interaction and research methods. The seven divisions provide an organized map of the work; they are not intended to impose absolute boundaries between subjects.

Research Structure

The current framework is organized into seven connected divisions. Each provides a primary home for a group of subjects, while cross-links connect topics that overlap.

I. Foundations — Origin and Origination

Origin, Consciousness / Freewill, imagination, origination and manifestation.

 II. Nature of Existence

Things, life, energy, matter, information, space and time.

 III. Structure — Oneness, Division and Boundaries

Principles, systems, relationships, boundaries, multiplicity and independence.

 IV. Origin Models — True Origin and Fake Origin

Origin models and the proposed distinction between True Origin and its imitation.

 V. Creation — Creation and Origination Mechanisms

Spontaneous and intentional imagination, conditional and programmed creation, and further origination.

 VI. Dynamics — Operation and Development

Interactions, causality, states, processes, cycles, transformation and possibility.

 VII. Research — Investigation and Verification

Perception, observation, measurement, uncertainty, knowledge and research records.

Research Approach

The project remains open to any methodology, discipline, perspective or investigative approach that may contribute to discovering the truth of Origin / the Ultimate Source. No single method or current body of knowledge sets the limits of exploration; the approach should be appropriate to the subject and question. The value of a method and its findings remains open to examination through its reasoning, observations, limitations and supporting evidence. Its foundational positions are set out in the Core Principles of Origin.

The project aims to make its concepts and reasoning clear enough to examine, question and refine. It seeks to distinguish:

  • Definitions and conceptual frameworks
  • Propositions and interpretations under consideration
  • Observations and recorded information
  • Evidence, analysis and conclusions
  • Open questions and areas requiring further investigation

Where a subject permits it, research may develop operational definitions, methods of observation and measurement, and ways to examine whether results can be reproduced. Not every foundational question is currently measurable, and a proposed explanation is not treated as established merely because it forms part of the framework.

Research Status

Science of Origin is an evolving research framework, not a completed or externally validated body of science. Some pages describe working concepts and proposed models; others organize questions, distinctions or possible methods for further investigation.

The presence of a subject in the framework does not mean that its propositions have been verified. Claims should be assessed according to the evidence and reasoning available for that specific subject.

An Evolving Project

The structure, terminology and subject coverage may change as the work develops. New questions may be added, existing concepts may be refined, and divisions may be reorganized when doing so improves clarity.

The purpose of the framework is to support exploration—not to close inquiry prematurely.

Working status: initial research framework. Content and organization remain open to review and revision.
