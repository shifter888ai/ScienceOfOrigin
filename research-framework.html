How the project is organized

Research Framework

An open structure for investigating Origin, origination, manifestations and their relationships.

Research scope

The project begins with three connected subjects:

  • Origin: the fundamental source.
  • Origination: the process of originating.
  • Manifestations: that which originates or is expressed.

The relationships among these subjects are themselves important research questions.

Conceptual models under consideration

0–1–2

A provisional representation discussed for organizing the relationship between source, proof and manifestation:

0 — Source · 1 — Proof · 2 — Manifestation

A–B–C

A second provisional representation:

  • A: Source
  • B: Manifestation
  • C: Proof or investigation connecting A and B

These models are working concepts, not fixed definitions or established scientific laws. They may be refined, combined, replaced or expanded.

Research principles

  • Remain open to new possibilities without prematurely treating them as conclusions.
  • Distinguish original ideas, interpretations, observations and established findings.
  • Develop research methods appropriate to each subject.
  • Use measurement, experimentation and reproducibility wherever they are meaningful and feasible.
  • Keep the framework expandable as new questions and fields emerge.

Relationship with Science of Consciousness

Science of Consciousness is a separate but related research project. Its existing work—including research into Consciousness, Freewill and Spectrum / Spectra—may inform or connect with Science of Origin, but does not define or limit the scope of Science of Origin.

Working status: This framework is provisional and will be revised as the research history is reviewed and the concepts develop.
