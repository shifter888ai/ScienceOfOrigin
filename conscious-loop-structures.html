Conscious-Loop Structures

An exploration of conscious-loop structures as underlying structures through which forms may be maintained, varied, interconnected, transformed and withdrawn.

The basic conscious-loop

A basic conscious-loop is described as an underlying structure necessary to hold a form stable. It is not itself a large structure. Many basic loops may be interconnected to form a larger loop or a more complex structure.

The lifespan of a conscious-loop depends on the type of structure designed or imagined. Some may be near-indefinite; others may be short-lived. There is no single lifespan assumed for every type.

A particle or an atom is offered as a basic example: in this framework, a basic conscious-loop wireframe is the underlying structure that holds a physical particle or atom together. This is a proposed interpretation within the Science of Origin account, not an established result of conventional physics.

Variations of a common basic structure

Different types of conscious-loop are understood as variations of a common basic loop, rather than entirely unrelated fundamental structures. Variations arise through both design and operating rules.

Within this framework, the 118 chemical elements known to humans are treated as representing 118 local variations of conscious-loop on Earth. They are not regarded as a fixed or exhaustive set of possible types. New variations may be introduced at any time, and existing variations may be withdrawn.

This proposed correspondence is a framework interpretation. The number of recognized chemical elements does not, by itself, establish the existence or properties of conscious-loop structures.

Change, self-modification and evolution

A conscious-loop may change its own design and operating rules, and another conscious structure may also modify them. Both forms of change are possible within the account.

A loop may evolve into a different type or remain the same type despite changes. Both possibilities are available at will. New types may also originate spontaneously, without being designed or modified by another loop.

When a loop type is withdrawn, its constituent loops may be reused or reorganized into other types at will. A constituent loop may also exist independently after separation from a larger loop.

Change is not limited to one direction or one permanent configuration. As expressed in the broader Origin inquiry: the only thing that does not change is change.

Interconnection and structural stability

Local conscious-loops and a higher-level consciousness framework both contribute to maintaining a complex structure. They coordinate and support one another. Loops may be interconnected directly or through connecting structures and channels.

In the sample human body-complex account, conscious-loops are described as distributed throughout all twelve bodies. This is an averaged sample structure, not a claim that every human has an identical body-complex.

Individual loops may be replaced, redesigned or reorganized without dissolving the overall body structure. The complete consciousness wireframe may also be redesigned or upgraded while continuity of individual identity is preserved within the model.

Zero-Energy Space and its underlying loop

The Zero-Energy Space account describes a special spatial structure with a self-cycling or closed-loop arrangement. Energy entering the structure is described as cycling permanently or near-permanently, with little or no leakage; additional energy may produce additional space, and multiple spaces may exchange and recirculate energy.

A clarification in the present framework distinguishes the Zero-Energy Space from its underlying conscious-loop: the conscious-loop is the basic structure that makes a Zero-Energy Space possible; the space itself is not the conscious-loop. Each Zero-Energy Space is understood to require its own underlying conscious-loop structure.

The Zero-Energy design is described in this account as the original basic loop-structure, general to the physical universe. The development described in the source proceeds from small spaces, to merged medium and larger spaces, and ultimately to what the account calls the Material World or Physical Phenomenal World.

These statements record the source account and its subsequent clarification within this framework. They are not presented as independently verified descriptions of physical cosmology.

Open research questions

  • What characteristics distinguish one conscious-loop variation from another?
  • How do design and operating rules jointly determine the stability and lifespan of a loop?
  • How do local loops and higher-level structures coordinate?
  • How can a structure be redesigned while preserving continuity?
  • What determines whether a loop remains independent or becomes interconnected?
  • How might new types originate, and how might existing types be withdrawn?
  • What relationships, if any, can be investigated between the proposed loop structures and observable physical phenomena?
Research status: This page records an evolving conceptual and experiential framework, including interpretations of the Zero-Energy Space account. The proposed relationship between conscious-loops, particles, atoms and chemical elements is not established by conventional physics or chemistry. The distinctions, observations and possible methods of investigation remain open to examination and refinement.
