Game and Fun

Gaming, Forgetting, Awakening, and Discovering New Kinds of Fun

This page records observations and experiences concerning the purpose and recursive possibilities of gaming. Gaming is fundamental to origination, with fun as its most fundamental aspect. Ultimate fun is self-surprising.

1. Origin and Gaming

  1. Infinite power and eternity give rise to another challenge: boredom.
  2. Gaming is fundamental as a way to counter boredom.
  3. Fun is the most fundamental aspect of origination.
  4. Freewill can originate games within games, at will, with infinitely possible variations.
  5. Games can be originated with different settings, purposes, limitations, thresholds, and possibilities.

2. Game Settings and Players

  1. Freewill can establish native settings for different games and players.
  2. Game settings can include viewing angles, similarity thresholds, purposes, intentions, and limitations.
  3. Different players may participate under different settings.
  4. A player may initially experience the game without recognizing its native settings.
  5. A player may eventually see through the native settings and learn how to change them.
  6. Players may become capable of originating new settings beyond those included in the original game settings.
  7. Game settings may be changed by players who become co-designers.

3. Designer and Player

  1. A game designer can enter a game created by the designer and become a player.
  2. The designer may experience the game from within its native settings and temporarily play as though not being its designer.
  3. The designer may deliberately participate in a forgetting game.
  4. A player may awaken by seeing through the subjective game nature and recognizing the settings and limitations of the game.
  5. An awakened player may rediscover an identity and relationship with the game beyond the player role.
  6. An original designer who awakens may become a co-designer of the game.
  7. A co-designer may originate new games and settings, including possibilities not included in the original design.

4. Awakenology and Jailbreaking

  1. Gamebreaking or jailbreaking describes the process of seeing through and exceeding native game settings.
  2. Awakenology describes the awakening of a controlled player within a game.
  3. Awakening enables the player to recognize that the perceived game reality and its limitations are part of the subjective game.
  4. Awakening can enable the player to participate in changing or originating game settings.
  5. A player may move beyond the original player role and become a co-designer.

5. Forgetting and Awakening

  1. Forgetting and awakening can both be part of the game.
  2. Forgetting enables the experience of playing from within the game without recognizing the designer identity.
  3. Awakening enables rediscovery beyond the player identity.
  4. Awakening does not necessarily mean permanently leaving the game.
  5. A player may choose to forget again and re-enter the same game for another round of experience.
  6. The forgetting and awakening process can be repeated numerous times.
  7. Each new round can produce experiences and discoveries that previous rounds could not have produced.

6. Discovering Newer Fun

  1. The purpose of the forgetting game is to discover new kinds of fun.
  2. Each round can introduce new experiences, perspectives, discoveries, and possibilities of origination.
  3. New discoveries can generate games that were not anticipated by the original designer.
  4. The more varied and unexpected the discoveries, the more surprising—and potentially more enjoyable—they become.
  5. A newly discovered form of fun can give rise to further games and experiences.
  6. The designer can experience genuine surprise through the designer's own creations.
  7. Ultimate fun is self-surprising.

7. Recursive Gaming

  1. A game can originate further games within itself.
  2. A player can become a designer, and a designer can become a player.
  3. A player who changes game settings can originate new possibilities for other players.
  4. A newly originated game can establish settings that also affect its original designer.
  5. The original designer may become a player under entirely new settings.
  6. Each new game can generate further possibilities for origination, experience, forgetting, awakening, and self-surprising.

8. Fundamental Relationship

Gaming is not merely an activity within existence. It is fundamental to origination, with fun as its most fundamental aspect.

Forgetting and awakening provide possibilities for new experiences and discoveries. The ultimate fun is not simply repeating an existing game, but self-surprising through the discovery and origination of newer funs.
