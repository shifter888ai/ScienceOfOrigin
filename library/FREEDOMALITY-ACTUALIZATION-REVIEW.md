# Freedomality and Actualization of Freewill — Source Review

**Date:** 2026-11-26  
**Status:** Source-comparison memo · Working research record  
**Scope:** Freedomality of Consciousness, Level of Actualization of Freewill, Freedomality Index (FI), Freedometry, and the source's uses of “actualization.”  
**Publication boundary:** Internal research only. No public terminology or glossary page is changed by this memo.

## 1. Purpose

This review checks whether the current project sources establish the relationship among Freedomality of Consciousness, Level of Actualization of Freewill, FI and Freedometry. It also distinguishes the actualization of free will from the actualization of a manifestation.

The review preserves source wording and document-level status. It does not create a numerical model or settle the terminology hierarchy.

## 2. Directly supported statements

### 2.1 Current internal Science of Consciousness framework

In `materials/awakenology/Science.md`, Section 5:

- Soul Nature is described as inherently 100% free will.
- Soul Characteristics may express only a portion of that free will.
- Freedomality of Consciousness is described as the degree to which an individual's inherent free will is actually expressed through their Soul Characteristics.
- Freedomality Index (FI) is described as the quantified representation or estimate of Freedomality of Consciousness.
- Freedometry is described as a proposed measurement methodology for operationalizing and estimating Freedomality of Consciousness and FI.

The same section explicitly leaves the FI scale, calculation, aggregation rules and interpretation to be operationally defined and validated. It assumes no fixed numerical scale and describes Freedometry as proposed, not as an established or validated measurement method.

The section also says a possible research approach is to examine observable signals and derived indicators using Spectrum of Consciousness assessment or sensor systems. It requires the relationship between those measurements and the actualization of free will to be explicitly defined, tested and independently validated. Direct observations must remain distinct from derived estimates and interpretations.

### 2.2 Historical project-term usage

The legacy repository-wide `TERMINOLOGY-INVENTORY.md` records **Level of Actualization of Freewill** as a previously adopted replacement term for “Freedom of Consciousness.”

This establishes historical usage within the project record. It does not, by itself, provide the primary defining passage or prove that the term is identical to Freedomality of Consciousness or FI.

The earlier `library/TERMINOLOGY-CONCEPTUAL-REVIEW-2.md` also records that the supplied framework documents did not define Level of Actualization of Freewill, FI or Freedometry. The current source comparison therefore retains the definition gap while adding the historical usage note.

### 2.3 Actualization of a manifestation

The current `consciousness-freewill.html` distinguishes:
- Intention as associated with Consciousness / Freewill.
- Origination as the capability through which an intention can become manifest.
- The relationship between intention and actualization as an open research subject.
- Intention and outcome as not necessarily having a simple one-to-one relationship.

The current `what-is-freewill.html` states that an intention for something to happen does not, by itself, mean that the intended happening must necessarily be actualized.

These passages concern the relationship between intention and a happening or manifestation. They do **not** define “Level of Actualization of Freewill.” The two uses of “actualization” must not be conflated.

## 3. Working distinctions to preserve

| Term | What the inspected source supports | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| **Freedomality of Consciousness** | A property/concept describing the degree to which inherent free will is expressed through Soul Characteristics | Full operational definition, observable indicators and validated measurement |
| **Level of Actualization of Freewill** | A historical project term recorded as a previously adopted replacement for “Freedom of Consciousness” | Primary definition, exact scope, whether it names a level/measure/property, and its relationship to Freedomality |
| **Freedomality Index (FI)** | A quantified representation or estimate of Freedomality of Consciousness | Scale, units, formula, aggregation, calibration, uncertainty and interpretation |
| **Freedometry** | A proposed methodology for operationalizing and estimating Freedomality and FI | Actual protocol, repeatability, validity and independent testing |
| **Actualization of a manifestation** | A research topic concerning how intention and origination relate to an outcome becoming manifest | Conditions, pathways, causal relationships and limits of control |

These are not five interchangeable names. The available sources do not establish that Level of Actualization of Freewill = Freedomality of Consciousness = FI. Nor do they establish that FI is the only possible measure of Level of Actualization.

## 4. Unresolved source concepts

### Soul Nature and Soul Characteristics

The current internal framework uses both terms as parts of its Freedomality description. In the reviewed passages, however, it does not provide their independent full definitions, boundaries or assessment criteria. They should remain source terms whose detailed histories need separate extraction.

### “100% free will”

The statement that Soul Nature is inherently 100% free will is a framework statement. The source does not specify in this passage whether “100%” is a measured score, a conceptual description of inherent nature, or a comparison baseline. Do not turn it into a calibrated measurement or assume it is directly comparable with a future FI score.

### Observation versus estimate

The framework explicitly requires a distinction between directly observed signals, derived indicators, estimates and interpretations. Any later measurement design should preserve these layers rather than treating a sensor output as a direct measurement of free will without a validated mapping.

## 5. Research questions retained

1. Where is the original passage that introduced or defined **Level of Actualization of Freewill**?
2. Was it intended to replace only the label “Freedom of Consciousness,” or also to redefine the underlying construct?
3. Is Level of Actualization of Freewill a property, a level, a measurement result, or a broader research subject?
4. Is Freedomality of Consciousness the construct being measured, while FI is one possible representation and Freedometry one proposed methodology? This is a possible reading of the current wording, not yet an approved equivalence.
5. What are the source definitions and boundaries of Soul Nature and Soul Characteristics?
6. What observable indicators, if any, can be linked to actualization of free will through a reproducible method?
7. How should actualization of free will be distinguished from the actualization of an intended manifestation?

## 6. Current research position

The most defensible source-based position is:

- Retain **Level of Actualization of Freewill** as a historically adopted project term.
- Retain **Freedomality of Consciousness**, **Freedomality Index (FI)** and **Freedometry** as separately named current framework terms.
- Do not declare these terms equivalent until the primary source history and intended construct relationship are located.
- Do not invent an FI range, formula, unit, scale or conversion.
- Keep “actualization of a manifestation” separate from the actualization of free will.
- Treat the 100% Soul Nature statement as source-framed, not as a calibrated measurement.
- Keep measurement validity, calibration, uncertainty, repeatability and independent validation open.
