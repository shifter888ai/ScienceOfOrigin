## 3. Overall structure and scoring as stated by the source

The overview says:

- The framework is divided into nine main areas and one auxiliary area.
- It reports approximately 1,800 main assessment points, several tens of thousands in total, and more than one hundred thousand subdivisions. These are source-reported figures, not independently counted here.
- Main-area scoring is not entirely percentage-based: lower stages tend toward percentage scoring, while higher stages may exceed that range and are described as unlimited.
- The source's later explanation of auxiliary sections says they use graded assessment from Level 1 (lowest) through Level 9. It explains that auxiliary items may apply to multiple main points and can vary by concrete case, so one single score may not summarize them adequately.

**Structural nuance:** The overview says “one auxiliary area,” while the detailed table of contents and body show auxiliary/supplementary subsections attached to several main areas. Both descriptions are preserved as written; this catalogue does not reconcile them into a new taxonomy.
