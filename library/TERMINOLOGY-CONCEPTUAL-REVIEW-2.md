## 1. Keep the research, method, subject, measurement and state categories distinct

### Current framework and supplied-document statements

The detailed supplied framework document lists four **Core Research Fields**:
1. Science of Consciousness.
2. Science of Freewill.
3. Spectrum of Consciousness.
4. Spectrum of Things.

It separately identifies **Spectrum / Spectra** as the core research methodology. It states that the primary objective is to investigate whether Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness, and explicitly says this remains a research objective rather than an established conclusion.

The short supplied architecture outline lists Science of Consciousness and Science of Freewill as Research Fields, then lists Spectrum / Spectra as the Core Research Methodology. It does not classify the two named spectra under its Research Fields heading.

The current public Science of Consciousness page uses a later arrangement: Science of Consciousness is the core research field; Spectrum / Spectra is the current methodology; Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Things are principal spectral research subjects. The page states that Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Freewill refer to the same Spectrum, but does not say that Spectrum of Things is the same Spectrum. The equality between Spectrum of Things and Spectrum of Consciousness remains an open objective.

### Historical Reddit wording

In a YWS-attributed Reddit answer dated 2024-09-28 (source lines 6432–6483):

- “The Level of Awareness = Lucidity of Consciousness.”
- Lucidity of Consciousness is described as quantifiably measurable through a methodology called “Stength of Consciousness (SoC)” or “Strength of Freewill (SoF)”. The source spelling “Stength” is retained in the source and inventory.
- It lists Strength of Freewill (SoF), Strength of Consciousness (SoC), and Spectrum of Consciousness (SoC) as terms used by different groups.
- It places these under the broader field of Science of Consciousness (SoC).
- It refers to “265 SoC Assessment Points or Developemnt Points”; the spelling is preserved as a source form.

### Review reading

The sources use several different conceptual levels. For comparison, retain these as separate working categories until the owner confirms their exact relationships:

| Category | Source terms | What the source supports |
|---|---|---|
| Research undertaking / science | Science of Consciousness; historical Science of Freewill | The umbrella field or historical research-field organization |
| Research methodology | Spectrum / Spectra; historical Strength of Consciousness / Strength of Freewill methodology wording | Named approaches described as methods or methodologies in particular source versions |
| Research subjects / spectra | Spectrum of Consciousness; Spectrum of Things; historical Spectrum of Freewill | Named spectral subjects; the current page equates Spectrum of Freewill with Spectrum of Consciousness only |
| Measurement expressions | Strength of Consciousness; Strength of Freewill | Historical source names for measurement-related methodologies or expressions; exact operational status remains unresolved |
| State / variable descriptions | Level of Awareness; Lucidity of Consciousness | One historical answer explicitly equates these two terms |
| Assessment structure | SoC Assessment Points / Development Points; Chinese major / minor assessment points and functional areas | Source-described assessment-point collections and functional groupings |

This is a classification aid, not a new official taxonomy. In particular, the historical Reddit wording does not prove that Strength and Spectrum are interchangeable, and the repeated abbreviation **SoC** does not make Science of Consciousness, Strength of Consciousness and Spectrum of Consciousness the same term.

### Distinctions to preserve

1. Science of Consciousness is not interchangeable with Spectrum of Consciousness.
2. Spectrum / Spectra is the current methodology in the public page; Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Things are research subjects.
3. Strength of Consciousness and Strength of Freewill are named in a historical source as methodologies, but their relationship to the current Spectrum / Spectra methodology is not established.
4. Level of Awareness and Lucidity of Consciousness are equated in one source answer; this does not establish their equivalence with Strength or Spectrum.
5. The current page equates Spectrum of Freewill with Spectrum of Consciousness, not Spectrum of Things.
6. Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness remains an objective for investigation, not a conclusion.

### Status

**Partly classified; operational relationships unresolved.** The current page, supplied framework documents and historical Reddit answer should remain separately traceable.

## 2. Historical Consciousness Strength framework — source claims and limits

### Page 300: 18-level model

Chinese compilation Page 300 (source lines 8697–8726) presents an 18-level model:
- Levels 1–10 are grouped as “未破幻”.
- Levels 11–14 are described as having different capacities to create illusions.
- Levels 15–17 are described in relation to the “experimental area” and different projection levels.
- Level 18 is labelled “万物本源” and described with “无限知、无限能、无限频率、无限权限”.
- The source explicitly cautions that “无限知无限能” does not mean “全知全能”.
- It states that level 11 in the 18-level system corresponds to level 9 in “实用意识强度”.

These are statements in a historical source model. The page does not establish that the model is a validated measurement scale or that its levels are directly comparable to other scales.

### Pages 306–316: assessment-point framework

Chinese compilation Pages 306–316 (source lines 8841–9082, labelled 2018–2019 third edition) describe a later assessment-point framework:
- Page 306 says there are hundreds of major assessment points and tens of thousands of minor points, accumulated through research as described by the source.
- It says an “意识强度检测仪” is mainly used for testing and that points are grouped into functional areas.
- Pages 306–313 describe the Central Area / Mental Control Area, with paired positive and negative percentage values.
- Page 307 gives the formulas: positive value = 100% − negative value; negative value = 100% − positive value.
- The source says these values are objective experimental quantities and are not directly related to ordinary human ideas of “good” and “bad”.
- Its first example pairs degree of automation with degree of autonomy, identifying autonomy with “自由意识（freewill）”.
- Pages 314–316 introduce the Upper Area / Autonomous Consciousness Area and further measures, including energy-use rate, cooperation, flexible switching, calm handling, resilience, disengagement from addiction, empathy, stillness in solitude and self-release.

The pages contain source-reported percentage ranges for human populations. Those ranges are historical claims in the source, not independent population statistics or validated measurements in this review.

Pages 312–313 include the source disclaimer that the material is for experience sharing and reference only, without warranty, and that redistribution is permitted provided the material is not modified. The disclaimer remains part of the provenance and should accompany quotations or reproductions from this source.

### Page 399: composite value and practical range

Chinese compilation Page 399 (source lines 11136–11155) says:
- The first nine functional areas contain more than 200 major assessment points.
- The Consciousness Strength level is a composite value of all assessment points.
- The points examine union-with-Origin and the depth of recognizing illusion from multiple angles.
- The source states that sufficient daily practice of the 200-plus items may expand an individual's Consciousness Strength to levels 9–13.

This page is a source-stated account of how the composite level is described. It does not provide the complete underlying data, test-retest reliability, calibration procedures, independent replication or a full operational specification in the passage reviewed here.

### Page 1669: later qualification

Chinese compilation Page 1669 (source lines 46764–46773), dated 2020-10-11, states:
- “合一本源的比例=意识强度检测值”.
- It says “貌似破9” corresponds to about 90% union-with-Origin.
- It says “理论来说” 100% union would be Consciousness Strength 18.
- It then says Consciousness Strength 18 = Origin and is forever unattainable, with a collective great spirit able only to approach 17.9999999…

The qualifiers “貌似” and “理论来说” are material and must be preserved. This later passage qualifies the Page 300 description of level 18 as 万物本源: one source describes level 18 as a category in the 18-level model, while the later source says a measured value of 18 is unattainable. These passages should remain distinct historical statements until the scale versions and their meanings are clarified.

### Page 264: union and liberation are distinct

Chinese compilation Page 264 (source lines 7431–7463), with an original-version date of 2008-08-25, states “得道=合一本源+脱离轮回”. It distinguishes seeing or experiencing union with Origin from personally verifying the claimed outcome of exiting reincarnation.

This historical passage is relevant to the application boundary: union-with-Origin and liberation from reincarnation are presented as combined but distinguishable elements, not automatically as synonyms. The source's experiential and metaphysical claims are preserved as source statements, not independently validated conclusions.

### Review reading

At least four layers appear in the historical material and should not be collapsed:

1. **Assessment points:** individual items or dimensions to be examined.
2. **Measurement procedure / instrument:** the source-described way of testing those points.
3. **Composite Consciousness Strength value or level:** a value said to combine multiple assessment points.
4. **Union-with-Origin / liberation interpretation:** the source's interpretation of what particular values or levels mean.

The 18-level model, the third-edition assessment-point framework, the Page 399 composite description, the Page 1669 later qualification and the 2024 Reddit answer may refer to related work, but their numerical counts and operational relationships are not demonstrated to be identical.

### Status

**Historical framework preserved; validation not established by the sources reviewed.** Keep the source claims, dates, version labels, qualifiers and disclaimers attached to the relevant passages. Do not present the reported percentages, levels, instruments or assessment-point totals as independently validated results.

## 3. Five application fields — scope supported by the supplied framework

Both supplied Science of Consciousness documents list the same five applications, and the current public page uses the same five names.

| Application | Supplied source description | What remains open |
|---|---|---|
| Freedomality of Consciousness | Freedomality as a property of consciousness, with possible measurement and quantification | Operational definition; relation to Freewill, Level of Actualization of Freewill, Freedomality Index and Freedometry |
| Entropy of Consciousness | Entropy in relation to consciousness, including properties, characteristics and measurable aspects | Operational definition and measurement; do not import a conventional entropy definition without a source-grounded mapping |
| Strength of Consciousness of Things | Strength of consciousness in relation to things, including properties and potential measurement | Relationship to Strength of Consciousness, Spectrum of Things and the proposed Spectrum equality |
| Awakenology | Applied research into methods of awakening humanity and development of consciousness | Scope and relation to the other application fields |
| Exincarnology | Applied research into methods of exincarnation (脱離輪迴), focused on liberation from the reincarnation cycle | Exact method scope and organizational relationship to Awakenology |

### Distinctions to preserve

1. **Strength of Consciousness of Things** is listed as an application field. It must not automatically be treated as identical to the historical **Strength of Consciousness** measurement expression.
2. **Entropy of Consciousness** is named as an application, but the supplied framework does not give an operational definition. Do not silently import the thermodynamic definition.
3. **Freedomality of Consciousness** is described as a property of consciousness and a possible measurement subject. The supplied file does not define its index, units or calculation.
4. **Awakenology** and **Exincarnology** are listed side-by-side as applications in both supplied files and on the current public page. These documents do not establish a parent/child hierarchy between them.
5. The supplied files do not define **Freedomality Index (FI)**, **Freedometry** or **Level of Actualization of Freewill**. Their definitions and relations require direct source verification.

### Status

**Application names and short scope descriptions are aligned across the supplied files and current public page.** Their measurement definitions and internal relationships remain open.
