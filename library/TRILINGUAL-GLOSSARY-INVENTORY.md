# Science of Origin — Trilingual Glossary Inventory

**Status:** Expanded source-derived glossary register · Version 0.2  
**Languages:** English · 繁體中文 · 日本語  
**Purpose:** A curated working explanation layer built from the Terminology Inventory. This is an internal research document, not the public Glossary. The published Glossary remains English-only.

## 1. How to read this file

- **Source statement** reproduces or translates what an inspected source says.
- **Working explanation** organizes the source material for research; it must not be mistaken for a verbatim source definition.
- **Translation** is editorial unless the source itself provides that language.
- **Unresolved** identifies a question that cannot yet be settled from the inspected sources.
- A formula or relation is not automatically a definition.
- Historical source statements are preserved by date and record number; later wording does not silently overwrite earlier wording.

## 2. Glossary entries

### Origin — 本源 — 本源（ほんげん）／根源

**Source statement (2013-11-16, Weibo record 106):**  
「本源=一个无限的思想。万物=思想的无限内容。本源之道=一个无限的思想及其无限的內容。」

**English rendering:**  
“Origin = an infinite thought. All Things = the infinite content of thought. The Way of Origin = an infinite thought and its infinite content.”

**Source statement (2022-04-02, Weibo record 569):**  
「本源=每一个存在的本质。」

**English rendering:**  
“Origin = the essence of each existence.”

**Working explanation:**  
The inspected source records preserve at least two distinct formulations of 本源: an “infinite thought” formulation in 2013 and an “essence of each existence” formulation in 2022. The 2021 post (record 384) further presents 本源 as inclusive of both sides of multiple oppositions and describes the complete Origin as 万物万事一体. These formulations should remain historically distinct until their relationship is reviewed.

**Traditional Chinese note:**  
本源的歷史表述包括「一個無限的思想」以及「每一個存在的本質」；兩者分別出現在不同年份的原始記錄中，不應在未經比較前合併為單一標準定義。

**日本語メモ:**  
確認できた原資料には、「無限の思想」（2013年）と「それぞれの存在の本質」（2022年）という異なる表現がある。両者を比較・検討するまでは、単一の定義に統合しない。

**Related terms:** 万物 / All Things; 万物本源 / All-Things Origin; 个体本源 / Individual Origin; 本源之道 / Way of Origin; 合一本源 / Union with Origin.

**Unresolved:** Whether these are complementary perspectives, developments in wording, or context-dependent descriptions requires broader source review.

**Sources:** Weibo records 106 (2013-11-16), 384 (2021-05-23), 569 (2022-04-02).

### All-Things Origin — 万物本源 — 万物の本源

**Source statements:**
- 2017-07-06, record 156: 「万物本源=个体本源+个体本源+个体本源+......」
- 2018-02-21, record 212: 「万物本源=万物本质。」
- 2022-04-02, record 569: 「万物本源=万物本质=万物整体。」

**English renderings:**
- “All-Things Origin = Individual Origin + Individual Origin + Individual Origin + …”
- “All-Things Origin = the essence of all things.”
- “All-Things Origin = the essence of all things = the totality of all things.”

**Working explanation:**  
The records provide multiple relations across time. Record 156 uses a repeated Individual Origin expression in an additive analogy/formula. Records 212 and 569 relate 万物本源 to 万物本质, with record 569 adding 万物整体. These are preserved as separate dated formulations, not compressed into a single definitive definition.

**繁體中文說明:**  
不同年份的原始記錄分別提出組合式表述，以及「萬物本源＝萬物本質」和「萬物本源＝萬物本質＝萬物整體」。應保留各自日期與原文，不宜直接合併。

**日本語メモ:**  
異なる時期の記録には、個体の本源を反復して示す関係式と、「万物の本源＝万物の本質」、さらに「万物の全体性」を加えた表現がある。各記録を日付ごとに保持する。

**Sources:** Weibo records 156, 212, 569.

**日本語メモ:**

**Sources:** Weibo records 129 (2015-01-11) and 580 (2022-05-01).

### Free Consciousness — 自由意识 — 自由意識

**Source statements:**
- 2018-04-12, record 230: 「自由意识是唯一的真实存在……自由意识=万物本源。」
- 2024-10-26, record 689: 「自然規律只有一個：自由意識（自由意志Freewill）.」

**Working explanation:**  
The 2018 post distinguishes 自由意识 as the only real existence from 自由意志法则 as the only real law, and also states 自由意识=万物本源. The 2024 post explicitly places “Freewill” in parentheses after 自由意识. Both source forms must be preserved. The parenthetical pairing in one post does not establish that every historical occurrence of 自由意识 and 自由意志 is interchangeable.

**繁體中文說明:**  
2018年的記錄將「自由意識」描述為唯一的真實存在，並將「自由意志法則」描述為唯一的真實法則；同一記錄另提出「自由意識＝萬物本源」。2024年的記錄則在「自由意識」後以括號標示「自由意志Freewill」。應保留各自語境，不把所有歷史用法一概視為同義詞。

**日本語メモ:**  
2018年の記録では「自由意識」と「自由意志の法則」が異なる役割で記述されている。2024年の記録では「自由意識」の後に「自由意志（Freewill）」が括弧書きされている。個々の文脈を保ち、全用例を一律に同義語とみなさない。

**Sources:** Weibo records 230 (2018-04-12) and 689 (2024-10-26).

### Attaining the Way — 得道 — 道を得ること

**Source statement (Chinese compilation, Page 264):**  
「亲眼见到的是合道（合一本源），亲身认证的是得道/证道（脱离轮回）。」  
「得道=合一本源+脱离轮回。」

**English rendering:**  
“What is seen directly is union with the Way (union with Origin); what is personally verified is attaining / realizing the Way (exiting the reincarnation cycle).”  
“Attaining the Way = union with Origin + exiting the reincarnation cycle.”

**Working explanation:**  
In this passage, 得道 is presented as involving two distinguishable elements: 合一本源 and 脱离轮回. The source distinguishes directly seeing/experiencing union with Origin from personally verifying the stated liberation outcome. “Attaining the Way” should not be reduced to either element alone.

**繁體中文說明:**  
第264頁將「得道」表述為「合一本源＋脫離輪迴」，並區分親眼見到的合一本源與親身認證的脫離輪迴。這兩者在該段落中是可區分的內容，不應合併成同一概念。

**日本語メモ:**  
第264頁では、「道を得ること」は「本源との合一」と「輪廻からの離脱」の二要素を含むと記述される。原文は、合一を直接見ることと、離脱を自ら確認することを区別している。

**Source:** Chinese compilation Page 264, lines 7431–7463.

### Exincarnation — 脫離輪迴 — 輪廻からの離脱

**Source statement:**  
Chinese compilation Page 264 uses 「脱离轮回」 as a distinct component of 得道. The supplied Science of Consciousness Project Description names “Exincarnation (脱離輪迴)” and describes Exincarnology as applied research into liberation from the reincarnation cycle.

**English rendering:**  
The current project term “Exincarnation” is mapped to the source phrase 「脱离轮回」. This English label is supplied by the current framework; it is not a literal English expression in the Chinese passage.

**Working explanation:**  
Exincarnation is recorded as the current project term for liberation from the reincarnation cycle. In Page 264 it is distinct from 合一本源 (Union with Origin), although the source presents both as components of 得道. This entry does not establish the mechanism or independently verify the claimed outcome.

**繁體中文說明:**  
目前研究框架將 Exincarnation 對應為「脫離輪迴」。第264頁將「脫離輪迴」與「合一本源」分別列為「得道」的兩個要素；本詞條保留此區別，不推定其實現機制或驗證其結果。

**日本語メモ:**
