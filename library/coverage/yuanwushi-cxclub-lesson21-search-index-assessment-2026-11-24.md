## Source-derived content summary

The lesson contrasts two patterns:

- **两头堵死**: inducement is described as drawing consciousness into a situation, while fear is described as preventing it from leaving; the result is restricted entry/exit and reduced autonomous choice.
- **两头开放**: the lesson describes restoring the ability to enter or not enter, continue or pause, and exit or move freely, with decisions made autonomously rather than under inducement- or fear-led pressure.

The lesson develops “意识精准度” (consciousness precision) through fine-grained and objective discernment. It asks readers to examine why they enter, why they do not exit, whether other options are visible, whether they can change a decision, and whether leaving one scene or level amounts to leaving the larger system.

It also introduces “100±1” as a framing pattern: a sequence of individually true statements may nevertheless steer a person toward an enlarged or diminished conclusion, while the person experiences the conclusion as independently reached. The lesson invites examples and real-life exercises. The closing summary emphasizes that the key is not which direction is chosen, but whether autonomous choice is genuinely available and hidden influence can be discerned.

These are the source's own teaching claims and conceptual framing. This assessment does not validate them as empirical findings.
