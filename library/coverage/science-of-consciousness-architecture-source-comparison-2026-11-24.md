## Architecture differences requiring care

### 1. Number and classification of core research fields

The detailed framework file, Section 3, lists four **Core Research Fields**, in this order:

1. Science of Consciousness
2. Science of Freewill
3. Spectrum of Consciousness
4. Spectrum of Things

The short architecture outline lists only two **Research Fields**:

1. Science of Consciousness
2. Science of Freewill

It does not list Spectrum of Consciousness or Spectrum of Things under that heading. Because the second file is a short outline, its omission may be abbreviation rather than a deliberate reclassification. The source does not say which.

The current public page presents Science of Consciousness as the research undertaking, treats Consciousness and Freewill as the same nature described by two human words, and says Science of Freewill is not maintained as a separate science. It describes Spectrum / Spectra as the methodology. The page does not currently present the four-item taxonomy from the detailed framework file.

### 2. Consciousness and Freewill

The current public page states that Consciousness and Freewill are the same nature of Origin described by two different human words, and that Science of Freewill is not a separate science from Science of Consciousness.

The supplied files list Science of Freewill as a research field, but neither supplied file explicitly states whether it is a separate science, a distinct research perspective within one science, or a historical/organizational label. Therefore, the difference is a confirmed classification discrepancy; it should not be expanded into a stronger disagreement about the underlying nature without clarification.

### 3. Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Things

The detailed framework file lists both spectra as core research fields and separately identifies Spectrum / Spectra as the core methodology. The current public page describes Spectrum / Spectra as the methodology and identifies the Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness relationship as an open research objective. It does not explicitly reproduce the four-field classification.

A useful distinction to preserve during any revision is:
- **Methodology:** Spectrum / Spectra.
- **Research subjects named in the detailed framework:** Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Things.
- **Open research objective:** investigate whether the two spectra are equivalent.

This distinction is present in the supplied detailed framework and is compatible with the current page's stated objective, but the formal category label (“core research field” versus “research subject”) is not yet aligned.

### 4. Applications

Both supplied documents list the same five applications. The current public page also lists all five. No discrepancy was found in this part of the architecture.

## Outstanding PR #159

PR #159 proposes:
- Changing the section heading to “Core research field: Science of Consciousness”.
- Adding a section that identifies Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Things as research subjects investigated through Spectrum / Spectra.

The proposal is directionally consistent with the current public page's treatment of Consciousness / Freewill and its Spectrum / Spectra methodology. However, it does not reproduce the detailed supplied document's four-item “Core Research Fields” taxonomy. It should not be merged as an automatic resolution of the newly supplied documents.

## Recommended handling

1. Preserve both supplied documents as separate source versions until their intended relationship is known.
2. Keep the current public page unchanged during this comparison.
3. Do not merge PR #159 or rewrite the architecture taxonomy solely on the basis of this comparison.
4. When the owner confirms the intended classification, update the public page and research directory in one focused change, while preserving the earlier arrangement in research history.
5. Keep the five applications and the Spectrum of Things = Spectrum of Consciousness objective visible in any revised architecture.

## Scope limitation

This is a document-structure and terminology comparison only. It does not validate the scientific claims, measurement methods, spectral definitions, or proposed equivalence in the source documents. No research finding has been added, removed, or upgraded in status.
