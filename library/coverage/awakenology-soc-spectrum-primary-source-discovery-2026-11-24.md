## Sources located

### 1. What Is SoC (Strength of Consciousness)

URL: https://www.awakenology.org/What-is-SoC-Strength-of-Consciousness/

- By YWS.
- Page identifies itself as the **5th Edition, 2018–2024**.
- A direct page open returned a readable text view (277 lines in the retrieved view). The view reports an old crawl age, while the search result for the same URL reports a much more recent crawl. Treat crawl metadata as inconsistent; do not infer a verified live revision date from it.
- The page states that two main data points are used in its account of escaping reincarnation: Strength of Consciousness / Spectrum of Consciousness level and DNA jail-breaking level.
- Section A explicitly uses “Strength of Consciousness”, “Spectrum of Consciousness” and “SoC” as equivalent names within this source's terminology. It describes an individual soul spectrum as a unique set of characteristics.
- Section B describes SoC levels as composite values formed from quantifiable assessment points and says the levels represent the breadth, freedom and autonomy of consciousness frequency. It also says a level is a range that may fluctuate rather than a fixed point.
- Section C relates the source's SoC levels to its three-tier Hypnotic Reincarnation model.
- Section H makes claims about scanner types and accuracy rates, including figures above 95%. These are claims in the source and have not been independently verified here.
- Section I says the source's committee studied hundreds of thousands of assessment points, with several hundred most commonly used, and that their comprehensive values form the SoC level.
- The page's own disclaimer says the content is for experience sharing and reference without guarantees, and that redistribution is allowed only if unchanged.

### 2. SoC AssessPoints (0) — Introduction

URL: https://www.awakenology.org/SoC-AssessPoints-0-Introduction/

- By YWS.
- Identifies itself as the **3rd Edition, 2018–2019**.
- The indexed text describes the soul's essence as consciousness and identifies free consciousness / Freewill with unrestricted autonomy in that source's framework.
- It distinguishes autonomous consciousness (not programmed or restricted by rules) from automatic consciousness (programmed and restricted by rules).
- It defines the strength of autonomy in consciousness as “Strength of Consciousness” (SoC), and says it can also be called “Spectrum of Consciousness” in technical terms.
- It states: Strength of Consciousness = Strength of Autonomy + Breadth of Consciousness.
- It describes a self-assessment example and says 265 SoC AssessPoints were published in 2018, with the total score reflecting the SoC level.
- A direct open attempt did not return the page body; the evidence currently available is the search-index text result. This is not a byte-faithful original-page capture.

## Research shout — suitable existing-topic connections

### Science of Consciousness / Spectrum of Consciousness

**Why useful:** The 3rd Edition introduction explicitly defines Strength of Consciousness as Strength of Autonomy plus Breadth of Consciousness and says it is also called Spectrum of Consciousness in technical terms. The 5th Edition continues to use Strength of Consciousness / Spectrum of Consciousness / SoC together. This is a direct historical source for the relationship between Strength and Spectrum.

**Suggested placement:** Historical terminology section for Strength of Consciousness / Spectrum of Consciousness; cross-reference the current distinction between Spectrum / Spectra as methodology and the named spectral research subjects. Preserve the edition/date labels.

### Science of Freewill

**Why useful:** The AssessPoints (0) and (1) sources connect Freewill with autonomy and contrast it with automatic consciousness. The first assessment area gives an explicit automation-versus-autonomy pair.

**Suggested placement:** Historical source record for Science of Freewill and later research into autonomous choice, while keeping the source's automatic/autonomous terminology distinct from any newer operational definitions.

### Freedomality of Consciousness

**Why useful:** The source operationalizes autonomy through paired assessment dimensions such as automation/autonomy, rigid/flexible response, and free/rigid thinking. These are candidate dimensions for comparison with the newer Freedomality of Consciousness application.

**Suggested placement:** Research-history cross-reference only. Do not adopt the 13 dimensions as the official Freedomality Index or Freedometry without a separate owner-approved operational mapping.

### Exincarnology / Hypnotic Reincarnation

**Why useful:** The 5th Edition SoC page directly relates its SoC levels to a three-tier Hypnotic Reincarnation model and separately names a DNA jail-breaking level. This is relevant historical source material for the project's reincarnation-related application research.

**Suggested placement:** Historical source reference under Hypnotic Reincarnation / Exincarnation. Keep the SoC and DNA data points distinct; do not infer that the source's full claims are validated or that DNA-related claims are part of the current method.

### Strength of Consciousness of Things

**Why useful:** The sources provide a historical account of Strength of Consciousness generally, but the pages reviewed here do not directly define Strength of Consciousness of Things.

**Suggested placement:** At most a background reference. Do not cite these pages as a direct definition or measurement specification for Strength of Consciousness of Things.

### Entropy of Consciousness

**Why useful:** No direct definition or operationalization was found in the pages reviewed.

**Suggested placement:** No reference recommendation from this source set at present.

## Important limitations

1. The pages contain extraordinary claims about non-human organizations, planetary systems, reincarnation operations, scanner technologies, accuracy rates, and DNA-related mechanisms. They must be attributed as claims made in YWS's source framework, not presented as established external facts.
2. The 5th Edition page includes level data described as updated through December 2023 and says the figures may continue to change. Do not present those values as current without a fresh source check.
3. The indexed SoC AssessPoints (0) and (1) results are not direct page captures. The underlying pages, images, diagrams, test-sample data and the remaining AssessPoints pages are not preserved by this note.
4. The Chinese compilation's disclaimers and page-level source context remain relevant when using the Chinese text. The English web pages have their own copyright and redistribution notices; retain original attribution and do not silently alter source wording.
5. No public research paper or reader-facing Glossary has been edited by this discovery note.
