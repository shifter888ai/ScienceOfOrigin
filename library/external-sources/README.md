# External Source Intake

**Purpose:** Register material discovered across the web without narrowing the research scope in advance.

This directory is an intake layer. It does not replace preserved source files, the source register, or the research library schema.

## How to add sources

The project owner can add source URLs in `INTAKE-TEMPLATE.csv`, or simply provide URLs in a ChatGPT conversation for assisted registration. Multiple URLs may be submitted together.

For each source, preserve the original URL and record enough information to relocate and identify the exact item. Do not assume that a search-result snippet, repost, mirror, or summary is the original source.

## Intake workflow

1. **Identify** — record the URL exactly as supplied and resolve redirects to a canonical URL where possible.
2. **Capture metadata** — title, author/creator, publisher/platform, publication date, update date, language, source type, and access date.
3. **Preserve** — capture the accessible source version and relevant attachments or media when technically and legally appropriate. Record capture method and limitations.
4. **Verify identity** — distinguish the original publication from mirrors, reposts, translations, citations, and commentary.
5. **Register** — assign a stable `SRC-` identifier only when the source object is formally registered.
6. **Link** — connect it to records, cases, terms, concepts, and research fields only after examining its content and context.
7. **Review** — record completeness, rights, privacy, provenance, and evidence status without silently changing the source.

## Important distinctions

- A URL is a locator, not proof that a source has been preserved.
- A search snippet is not a source capture.
- A later copy or mirror is not automatically the original.
- A translation, transcript, OCR text, or summary is a derived object linked to its source.
- A source's claims remain source claims until separately assessed.
- A source may be relevant to multiple topics—or to a topic not yet identified.
- Public accessibility does not automatically permit republication or unrestricted redistribution.

## Intake statuses

Use explicit statuses such as:

- `url-submitted`
- `metadata-captured`
- `source-accessible`
- `capture-complete`
- `capture-partial`
- `access-restricted`
- `unavailable`
- `rights-review-required`
- `registered`

Do not mark a source as preserved or registered merely because its URL has been listed.

## Scope

This intake is open to webpages, research papers, books and book chapters, forums, social posts, videos, audio, images, datasets, archived pages, and other material. Do not exclude an item solely because its apparent subject seems unrelated to current research topics.
