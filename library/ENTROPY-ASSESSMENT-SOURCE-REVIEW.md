## 2. Overall architecture and figures

The source overview states:
- The framework is organized into **nine main areas** and refers to **one auxiliary area**.
- It reports approximately **1,800 main assessment points**, several tens of thousands in total, and over one hundred thousand subdivisions.
- It describes the structure metaphorically as a multidimensional large sphere made of irregular luminous lines, with those lines connecting to and developing alongside regular lines.
- Its stated scoring approach is **not wholly percentage-based**: lower stages tend toward percentage scoring, while higher stages can exceed that range and are described as unlimited.

These totals and structural descriptions are source-reported claims. This review does not independently count all underlying points or validate the described mechanism. The TOC itself shows multiple attached `附属区` sections under different main areas, while the overview uses the singular phrase `1个副区`. This source-level wording difference is retained rather than reconciled.

- **Entropy autonomy:** target alignment and efficiency of autonomy.

- **Dynamic resource use:** discovering, using/mobilizing, integrating, and coordinating resources, plus follow-on improvement and experience development.
- **Flexible differentiation:** reaction speed, detail, richness, flexibility and ability to return to an integrated form.
- **Particle stacking efficiency:** overall utilization, residual rate, separation/output rate and waste rate.

## 5. Directly inspected explanatory examples

The following examples were read from the RTF body, not inferred from headings alone:

### Zone 1 — Multidimensional capability

The source says the central feature of the entropy assessment is multidimensional or cross-dimensional capability: observing people, events and things from multiple dimensions, and using multidimensional perspectives in thinking, action and emotion. It frames this as a basis or first step for breaking what it calls `规则幻`.

A separate point, `多维边界清晰度`, is explained in terms of clearer dimensional boundaries supporting more precise navigation and positioning.

### Zone 2 — Foundational characteristics

The point `动态查找的能力` is explained as continuously searching for new targets during dynamic activity and looking for continued development. The passage associates stronger capability with greater development potential, more possibilities and more unpredictability. This is the source's own conceptual framing.

### Zone 3 — Entropy dynamics

The source explains `动态中的分形能力` through the creation of many smaller perspectives during dynamic change. Each smaller perspective can be assigned an exploratory purpose, matched to the situation and needs, and later have its experience recovered.

### Zone 4 — Flexibility and particle behaviour

The source distinguishes `熵值粒子的自身震动的稳定性` from another earlier stability point: this auxiliary point concerns maintaining a basic vibration/frequency despite external interference and is described as a foundational property.

For `熵值粒子的记忆程度`, the source discusses whether a particle can retain its own target during continuing multidimensional change, including possible examination of when memory loss occurs, under what characteristics or dimensions, the proportion of loss and its effects.

### Zone 5 — Integration and collaboration

The source explains `熵值粒子联合中的开放度` as an underlying condition associated with a greater range of possibilities and broader development. It separately discusses transmission of information with `本源` during particle collaboration.

### Zone 6 — Laboratory basic tests

The source describes `熵值粒子弹性程度` as stretchability/expandability under temporary compression and expansion. It says laboratory conditions can simulate compression and expansion. It also describes multiple possible forms of elasticity and frames recovery as part of the temporary response being assessed.

For `熵值粒子防阻能力`, the source describes introducing additional resistance temporarily in a laboratory to observe the particle's ability to deal with it.

For `熵值粒子轮摆程度`, the source explicitly distinguishes a controlled oscillation test from the earlier general swing/oscillation point. It says the test applies control during movement and observes the resulting change. The passage also says there is no simple good/bad ranking: suitability depends on different purposes.

### Zone 9 — Practical activity

The source distinguishes real-time review during practice from reviewing only after practice. It also distinguishes real-time feedback as smaller and more immediate, describing it as a basis/basic skill for the broader real-time review process.

The auxiliary point `实践中的实时校准能力` is described as calibrating the overall direction at each step. The source uses the analogy of zooming out while drawing on an iPad to check the overall result after each stroke.

## 6. Assessment labels, scores and comparability

The body repeatedly uses the labels `RR`, `88` and `JJ` followed by numerical values/ranges and explanatory comments. The source does not define these labels in the inspected overview passage, so this review preserves them literally.

The values are heterogeneous. Examples include:
- Early multidimensional entries with RR values such as `30–60`, `30`, `40` and `60`, alongside values under `88` and `JJ`.
- Some entries with values in the hundreds or over one thousand.
- Laboratory and auxiliary entries expressed in levels such as `2级`, `6级` or ranges of levels.

The document's own overview says the scale is not wholly percentage-based and may be unlimited at higher stages. The body also includes a statement that many detection points are primarily used in laboratory testing because practical situations contain many variables; laboratory values are therefore described as reference-only and potentially different from actual practical performance.

**Do not normalize, compare or aggregate these values as though they shared a verified common unit.** Before any quantitative analysis, the project would need the source-defined meanings of RR/88/JJ, the scale/range for each point, the direction of better/worse where applicable, test conditions, calibration rules, and the relationship between laboratory reference and practical results.

## 7. Relationship to the public Entropy of Consciousness framework

The public `entropy-of-consciousness.html` introduction presents a nine-zone architecture and explicitly distinguishes laboratory reference assessments from practical performance. The RTF supplies substantially more detailed source material beneath that architecture.

The broad relationship is supported by the shared nine-zone structure. However:
- The RTF is a specific assessment document, not a complete definition of Entropy of Consciousness.
- The public page intentionally does not disclose detailed detection points or individual assessment records.
- The RTF's source-specific descriptions should not automatically be converted into general scientific definitions.
- The term `Entropy Particle` is used in the RTF; its full ontology and operational status are not independently established by this extraction.
