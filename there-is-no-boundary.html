There Is No Boundary

An Exploration of Apparent Boundaries as Virtual and Breakable

Initial research account · Research is ongoing

Abstract

There exists no boundary at all, as observed through observation and experimentation.

What appear to be boundaries are virtual and breakable. They may appear to distinguish, separate, contain or limit things, but they do not constitute absolute boundaries.

This paper presents the current understanding of Boundary within Science of Origin and explores the distinction between apparent boundaries and the absence of any actual boundary.

1. Introduction

The concept of boundary is commonly used to describe the separation or limitation of things, systems, spaces and domains.

However, observation and experimentation lead to a different understanding:

There exists no boundary at all.

All apparent boundaries are virtual and breakable. What appears to be a boundary should therefore not automatically be understood as an actual or absolute division.

This perspective invites a deeper examination of what humans perceive as boundaries and whether any apparent boundary can establish genuine separation.

2. The Nature of Boundary

A boundary may appear in many forms. It may be perceived as a physical edge, a spatial limit, a division between systems, a separation between entities or a restriction within a particular environment.

These appearances do not establish the existence of an actual boundary.

Within the current understanding of Science of Origin, no boundary exists at all. Apparent boundaries are virtual rather than fundamental.

The distinction is important: something may appear to have a limit or separation without that appearance representing an actual boundary in existence.

3. Apparent Boundaries Are Virtual

A virtual boundary is an apparent distinction or limitation that may be experienced or recognized as a boundary, without constituting an actual boundary.

The appearance of a boundary does not establish that something is fundamentally separated from something else.

Likewise, an apparent division does not establish that the things being distinguished are disconnected or exist independently.

The recognition of differences and distinctions should not be confused with the existence of boundaries.

4. Apparent Boundaries Are Breakable

All apparent boundaries are breakable.

A boundary that can be broken cannot establish an absolute and fundamental separation.

Its apparent effectiveness or persistence does not make it an ultimate boundary.

This understanding challenges the assumption that an apparent limit necessarily represents an unbreakable restriction or an actual division.

The specific ways in which different apparent boundaries manifest and can be broken remain subjects for further observation and experimentation.

5. Boundary and One Consciousness

There is only ONE Consciousness.

Consciousness is infinite and has no boundary at all. It is never divided or separated.

All spirits, souls, life forms and things exist within this One Consciousness.

Their individuality and differences do not create boundaries within Consciousness. Apparent distinctions do not divide the One Consciousness into separate parts.

The absence of boundaries is therefore consistent with the understanding of Consciousness as infinite, undivided and boundaryless.

6. Boundary and Individuality

The absence of boundaries does not mean that all manifestations must be identical.

Different focal points, souls, life forms and things may have their own characteristics and individuality while existing within the One Consciousness.

Individuality does not require separation, and differences do not establish boundaries.

This distinction is important when examining the relationship between individual manifestations and the One Consciousness.

7. Further Research

The understanding presented here provides a basis for further investigation into apparent boundaries and their manifestations.

  • The nature and characteristics of apparent boundaries.
  • How virtual boundaries manifest and are perceived.
  • The ways in which apparent boundaries can be broken.
  • The relationship between apparent boundaries and perceived separation.
  • The relationship between boundaries, individuality and the One Consciousness.
  • Whether additional observations and experiments reveal further characteristics of apparent boundaries.

These areas remain open to further observation, experimentation and discovery.

8. Summary

There exists no boundary at all, as observed through observation and experimentation.

All apparent boundaries are virtual and breakable. Their appearance does not establish actual or absolute separation.

Individuality and differences can exist without boundaries.

The One Consciousness remains infinite, undivided and boundaryless.

This is all that is being made available for now.
