What Is Frequency and Spectrum?

An Exploration of Frequency, Spectrum and Manifestation

Public research exploration · Research is ongoing

Abstract

This paper explores the nature of Frequency and Spectrum within the research framework of Science of Origin, particularly Science of Consciousness.

It examines Frequency as a manifestation, Spectrum as the complete composition of Frequencies associated with an existence, and the relationships among Frequencies, Spectra, Origin, thought, intention, imagination, self-awareness and Freewill.

The exploration considers individual and collective manifestations, the dynamic nature of Frequencies and Spectra, interactions within and between Spectra, and the relationship between individual manifestations and the unified nature of Consciousness and Freewill.

The purpose is to develop a coherent conceptual foundation for further investigation through Spectrum / Spectra research. The descriptions presented here provide a basis for continued exploration; measurement methods, experimental procedures and reproducible findings remain subjects for further research.

1. Introduction

What is Frequency?

What is Spectrum?

These questions are fundamental to investigating the nature of Life and Things through Science of Consciousness.

Frequency and Spectrum are commonly used as human terms to describe phenomena. However, the terms themselves should not be mistaken for the actual nature of what they describe.

This research explores Frequency and Spectrum from the perspective that every existence is a manifestation, and that Frequencies and Spectra provide ways to describe these manifestations and their compositions.

The exploration does not begin by assuming that conventional scientific descriptions of frequency fully represent the nature of Frequency being investigated.

Instead, it examines the characteristics, relationships and possibilities associated with Frequency and Spectrum through an open research approach.

2. Origin, Movement and Frequency

There is only ONE Freewill, which is Origin.

Origin continuously moves through thought, intention and imagination. Such movement is often described by humans as a vibration or Frequency.

A movement, vibration or Frequency represents a movement of thought, intention or imagination.

Frequency is therefore a human-made term used to describe such movement and manifestation. The term itself may not fully represent the actual nature of what it describes.

All existences are imaginations within the only One Freewill. Their manifestations are expressions of this continuous movement of Origin.

There is no fundamental Frequency. All Frequencies are equal.

3. Frequency as a Manifestation

A Frequency is a manifestation.

Frequency is not merely a measurement, signal or abstract value. Within this research framework, a Frequency is also a life form, with no fundamental difference in nature from other life forms.

A Frequency is something an existence has and something through which an existence manifests.

An existence can have multiple Frequencies, and each Frequency represents a distinct manifestation.

Every existence has a unique Frequency identity and Spectrum.

4. Spectrum as the Complete Composition of Frequencies

A Spectrum describes the complete composition of Frequencies associated with an existence.

It is not limited to a particular selection of Frequencies or merely the portion that can currently be detected or measured.

The complete Spectrum and the currently detectable portion of a Spectrum should be distinguished.

A Spectrum is not a fixed composition. Its composition can change dynamically.

Frequencies may emerge, disappear, transform or remain within a Spectrum without currently manifesting.

Consequently, a Spectrum can expand or contract as its composition changes.

The identity of an existence can be described through the complete composition of its Spectrum at a given moment. Whether different Spectrum compositions represent different identities or the continuation of one identity depends on the human-made definition being applied.

5. Frequency and Spectrum as Descriptions of Manifestations

A manifestation can be described as either a Frequency or a Spectrum, depending on whether it is considered as a single Frequency or a collective of Frequencies.

A single manifestation can therefore be described as a Frequency.

A collective manifestation composed of multiple Frequencies can be described as a Spectrum.

These descriptions are not necessarily different kinds of existence. They describe manifestations at different levels of composition.

A collective manifestation can itself be considered a single manifestation in another context. Accordingly, what is described as a Spectrum at one level can be described as a Frequency when considered as one manifestation within another Spectrum.

This relationship allows individual and collective manifestations to be examined within the same research framework.

6. Frequency Identity and Presence Across Spectra

A Frequency can be present in multiple Spectra simultaneously.

When the same Frequency is present in different Spectra, it remains exactly the same Frequency and has the same manifestation in those Spectra.

Its presence in multiple Spectra does not imply that it is duplicated or that separate copies have been created.

If that Frequency changes, it becomes another Frequency.

The relationship between a Frequency and a Spectrum should therefore not be understood as exclusive ownership or membership. A Frequency can appear in multiple Spectra without being restricted to only one.

7. The Dynamic Nature of Frequencies and Spectra

Frequencies and Spectra are dynamic rather than permanently fixed.

Frequencies can:

  • Exist simultaneously.
  • Emerge or cease to exist.
  • Transform into other Frequencies.
  • Remain within a Spectrum without manifesting.
  • Change their relationships with other Frequencies.
  • Influence the composition of their own Spectrum.

A Frequency can intentionally influence its own Spectrum, including its expansion or contraction and the introduction, disappearance or transformation of other Frequencies.

However, intention does not necessarily produce the intended result. An intended change may or may not be actualized, and the possible reasons are not restricted to any predetermined category.

Entropy can also change or remain constant while a Spectrum changes.

8. Self-Awareness and Freewill of Frequencies

There is only ONE Consciousness and ONE Freewill.

All existences are imaginations within the only One Freewill.

Self-awareness is intrinsic to Freewill itself. Freewill continuously imagines manifestations through movements of thought, intention and imagination.

A Frequency, as a manifestation representing such movement, has its own individual self-awareness while simultaneously expressing the unified self-awareness of the One Freewill.

Each Frequency can exercise its own Freewill independently, without being a separate ultimate source or being divided from the One Freewill.

A Frequency can be aware of:

  • Its own manifestation.
  • Other Frequencies.
  • Its complete Spectrum.
  • Other Spectra.

Awareness does not necessarily require conscious recognition or understanding.

A Frequency can also intentionally choose not to interact with another Frequency while remaining aware of it.

Awareness and interaction are therefore distinguishable aspects of the relationship between Frequencies.

9. Frequency Interaction

Frequencies within a Spectrum can have different forms of interaction.

They can:

  • Influence one another.
  • Mutually influence each other.
  • Cooperate or interact without changing one another.
  • Manifest independently while remaining present within the same Spectrum.

Interaction does not necessarily produce change.

When Frequencies interact, possible outcomes include one Frequency changing, both Frequencies changing, or other outcomes depending on the interaction.

Awareness of an interaction can be present even when a Frequency does not consciously recognize or understand that interaction.

A Frequency can also remain aware of other Frequencies without interacting with them.

10. Inter-Spectrum Relationships

Interactions are not limited to Frequencies within the same Spectrum.

A Frequency can intentionally influence another existence's Spectrum, including introducing, transforming or eliminating Frequencies.

Influence between Spectra can be reciprocal or one-way.

One existence can intentionally influence multiple Spectra simultaneously, with different outcomes in each.

Multiple Frequencies from different Spectra can also cooperate intentionally to influence another Spectrum.

These possibilities provide further areas for investigating the relationships between individual manifestations and collective manifestations.

11. Entropy and Frequency Characteristics

Within Science of Consciousness, Entropy of Consciousness is an important research field.

The fundamental nature of a life form and a thing is the same: Consciousness. The difference between them is the Entropy of their conscious structures.

This distinction is relevant to the investigation of Frequencies because Frequencies can have different Entropy levels.

Differences in Entropy can distinguish characteristics, capabilities and manifestations.

Entropy and Spectrum should nevertheless be examined as distinct aspects of research. A change in Spectrum does not necessarily mean that Entropy has changed, and Entropy can change while the Spectrum also changes.

The relationship between these aspects requires further investigation and appropriate methods of measurement.

12. Spectrum / Spectra as a Research Methodology

Spectrum / Spectra is the core research methodology of Science of Consciousness.

Its purpose is to investigate the complete composition of Frequencies, their characteristics, their relationships and their manifestations.

The research should distinguish between:

  • The complete Spectrum.
  • The portion currently detectable.
  • The capabilities and sensitivity of the detection instruments or systems.
  • The methods used for detection and measurement.
  • The interpretation of the observations obtained.

The limitations of a detection method should not automatically be treated as limitations of the actual Spectrum.

Further research should develop suitable approaches to identify, examine, measure and compare Frequencies and Spectra.

Where appropriate, research should progress from ideas to concepts and then to proof of concept through suitable measurement methods.

Practical investigation, quantifiable parameters, experimentation and reproducibility are important objectives.

13. Relationship to Shared Intentions

Shared Intentions is a separate research topic within Science of Origin.

It is not exclusive to Frequency or Spectrum research and can apply across different research fields.

However, the exploration of Frequency and Spectrum provides one context in which shared intentions can be examined.

A shared intention can itself be described as a Frequency when considered as a single manifestation, or as a Spectrum when considered as a collective of Frequencies.

The detailed investigation of individual intentions and shared intentions will be developed separately in the papers What Is Intention? and Shared Intentions.

14. Further Research

The exploration presented in this paper opens several areas for continued research:

  1. Further examination of the nature of Frequency.
  2. Investigation of the complete composition and dynamic characteristics of Spectra.
  3. Examination of Frequency identity and the continuity of manifestations.
  4. Investigation of the relationship between Origin, thought, intention, imagination and Frequency.
  5. Investigation of self-awareness and Freewill in individual Frequencies.
  6. Study of interactions within and between Spectra.
  7. Investigation of the relationship between Entropy and Spectrum.
  8. Development of suitable methods for detecting and measuring Frequencies and Spectra.
  9. Experimental examination of the relationships among individual and collective manifestations.
  10. Development of reproducible methods for Spectrum / Spectra research.

These areas remain open for further investigation, development and discovery.

15. Summary

This paper has explored Frequency and Spectrum as interconnected ways of describing manifestations and their compositions.

Origin continuously moves through thought, intention and imagination. Such movement is often described by humans as vibration or Frequency.

A Frequency is a manifestation, while a Spectrum describes the complete composition of Frequencies associated with an existence.

A manifestation can be described as a Frequency or a Spectrum depending on whether it is considered individually or collectively.

Frequencies and Spectra are dynamic. Their compositions and relationships can change, while individual Frequencies retain their identities when present in multiple Spectra.

The exploration also examines the individual self-awareness and Freewill of Frequencies within the unified nature of the One Consciousness and One Freewill.

These descriptions provide a basis for continued investigation through Spectrum / Spectra methodology. Further exploration, measurement, experimentation and discovery can expand and develop the understanding presented here.

Research is ongoing. The exploration of Origin has no final conclusion.

Research Status

This paper presents an exploratory conceptual framework for the investigation of Frequency and Spectrum within Science of Origin.

The descriptions and propositions presented here are intended to guide continuing research. They should not be mistaken for experimentally established findings where such evidence has not yet been provided.

This is all that is being made available for now.
