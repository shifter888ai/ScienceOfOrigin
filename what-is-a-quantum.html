What Is a Quantum?

An Exploration of the Minimal Conscious Structure of a Universe

Initial research account · Research is ongoing

Abstract

A quantum is the minimal conscious structure and minimal particle of a universe, originated by Origin.

Each individual quantum is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill. It serves as the minimal construction material for forming a universe.

Each universe contains exclusively its own specific type of quantum, associated with the characteristics of that universe.

This paper presents the current understanding of Quantum within Science of Origin, based on observation and experimentation, and identifies questions that remain under research.

1. Introduction

The term quantum is already used in human physics. However, the understanding presented in this paper explores a deeper level of the quantum concept through observation and experimentation within Science of Origin.

A quantum is understood as:

  • A minimal conscious structure.
  • A minimal particle of a universe.
  • A structure originated by Origin.
  • A conscious entity that is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.
  • The minimal construction material for forming a universe.

This understanding places Quantum within the relationship:

Origin → Quantum → Universe

2. The Nature of Quantum

A quantum is the minimal conscious structure originated by Origin.

It is also the minimal particle of a universe.

The term therefore describes more than a minimal construction particle. Its nature is conscious, and its self-awareness is intrinsic.

Each individual quantum is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

Self-awareness is not something that emerges only when multiple quanta combine. It is already present in each individual quantum.

3. Quantum and Freewill

Each individual quantum is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

This understanding is consistent with the broader research position that self-awareness is intrinsic to Freewill.

A quantum is therefore not merely an unconscious construction material. Its individual nature includes self-awareness and Freewill.

The relationship between the individual self-awareness of a quantum and the wider universe it helps form remains an area for further investigation.

4. Quantum as the Minimal Construction Material of a Universe

A quantum serves as the minimal construction material for forming a universe.

It is the minimal particle from which a universe is constructed.

The basic structure of a quantum is designed by the first designer(s) of that universe. This structure may be modified or upgraded at any later stage.

This gives Quantum a fundamental position in investigating the formation and characteristics of universes.

The relationship can be expressed as:

Origin → Quantum → Universe

Origin is the source of the quantum. Quantum is the minimal conscious construction material. A universe is formed through its quantum composition.

Further details of the formation process remain open for investigation.

5. Quantum Types and Universe Characteristics

Each universe is filled exclusively with its own specific type of quantum.

The specific quantum type is associated with the characteristics of that universe.

Different universes therefore have their own corresponding quantum types.

This understanding identifies a relationship between the type of quantum and the characteristics of the universe it forms.

Whether different types of quantum can coexist within a single universe remains under research. It is not presented here as an established finding.

6. Quantum and Human Physics

The use of the term Quantum in this research should not be assumed to be identical to the quantum concept in human physics.

Human physics provides its own observations, terminology and descriptions of quantum phenomena.

The understanding presented here investigates a deeper level through the perspective of Science of Origin: Quantum as a minimal conscious structure, self-aware as Freewill, and the minimal particle and construction material of a universe.

The relationship between this understanding and the quantum concepts developed in human physics may provide an area for future comparison and investigation.

7. Further Research

The current understanding establishes several directions for continued research:

  • The characteristics and nature of an individual quantum.
  • The intrinsic self-awareness of each quantum as Freewill.
  • The relationship between Quantum and Origin.
  • How quanta form a universe.
  • The relationship between a universe's quantum type and its characteristics.
  • The characteristics that distinguish different quantum types.
  • Whether different types of quantum can coexist within one universe.
  • The relationship between Quantum, Consciousness, Freewill and Focal Points.
  • The possible relationship between this understanding and quantum concepts in human physics.

The question of whether different quantum types can coexist within one universe remains under research.

These areas remain open to further observation, experimentation and discovery.

8. Summary

A quantum is the minimal conscious structure and minimal particle of a universe, originated by Origin.

Each individual quantum is self-aware as Freewill and is Freewill.

Quantum serves as the minimal construction material for forming a universe.

The basic structure of a quantum is designed by the first designer(s) of that universe and may be modified or upgraded at any later stage.

Each universe contains exclusively its own specific type of quantum, associated with the characteristics of that universe.

Whether different types of quantum can coexist within one universe remains under research.

This is all that is being made available for now.
