Terminology

A working reference to the terms and distinctions used in Science of Origin. It records how terms are currently being used, how they relate, and where their meanings still need clarification.

Nothing here is fixed

Everything is evolving; nothing is fixed. This page is not a final dictionary, a closed vocabulary or an authority that limits future inquiry. Terms and their descriptions may be expanded, refined, reorganized or replaced as observations, experiences, investigations and understandings develop.

The purpose is to make current language easier to examine and discuss. A working meaning describes how a term is being used at this stage; it does not claim to capture the complete nature of what it refers to. Where a meaning is uncertain or incomplete, that should remain visible rather than being concealed by an artificially precise definition.

Science of Origin shares its observations, experiences and research for reference, not instruction. Readers are invited to examine the material independently and reach their own conclusions.

How to read this reference

  • Working meaning — the current use of a term within this developing framework, not a permanent or exhaustive definition.
  • Distinction — a difference that matters when examining related terms. Similar or connected terms should not automatically be treated as interchangeable.
  • Relationship — a connection proposed or explored between terms. A listed relationship does not, by itself, establish proof, causation or experimental confirmation.
  • Open questions — aspects whose meaning, scope or implications remain to be clarified or investigated.

The entries below preserve the current framework's language and distinctions. They are an initial selection, not a complete vocabulary. Their order is for reference and does not imply a sequence, hierarchy of importance or hierarchy of evidence.

1. Origin and Infinity

Origin

Working meaning: Origin refers to the Ultimate Source, described in this framework as the Oneness Origin. It is not merely a beginning in time, an event at the start of a timeline or an object among other objects.

Distinctions: Origin and origination are related but different. Origin concerns the fundamental source; origination concerns the capability and process through which manifestations come about. Origin is not one manifestation among others.

Related perspectives: Infinity is the closest description of Origin that humans can understand, but it is not a definition of Origin. Spirit describes its nature; Consciousness describes its attributes; Freewill describes its functionality; Voidness is a visual perspective on the Source in stillness and non-manifestation. These are approximate perspectives on the same Source, not separate entities, interchangeable definitions or exhaustive descriptions.

Open questions: The language used for Origin cannot claim to define or exhaust it. The relationships among the descriptive perspectives, imagination, origination and manifestation remain open to further clarification.

Origin · Core Principles of Origin

Infinity

Working meaning: Infinity is the closest description of Origin that humans can understand, but it is not a definition of Origin. It is also a key research domain within Science of Origin. It does not mean merely an extremely large quantity, an endless numerical sequence or an unlimited duration. It refers to the infinite nature associated with the Ultimate Source.

Distinctions: Infinity itself is not treated as a numerical quantity or as something to be directly measured. Particular manifestations, observable relationships and implications associated with Infinity may be investigated without claiming to measure or exhaust Infinity.

Relationship: The framework also uses Infinity in describing Zero as the idle state of Origin and Infinity before manifestation. The phrase “before manifestation” is not automatically chronological.

Open questions: How Infinity relates to possibility, imagination, manifestation and the unknown remains an ongoing subject of inquiry. Even the current description remains open to refinement.

2. Zero and Idle

Zero

Working meaning: Zero represents the idle state of Origin and Infinity before manifestation. In this framework, “0 = Infinity” is an observed understanding, not an arithmetic equation or a numerical starting point.

Distinctions: Zero is not used here as the number from which counting begins, a mathematical origin, a physical vacuum, simple nothingness, or a moment on a timeline. The term concerns Origin rather than a quantity or object within an already manifested system.

Relationship: Zero, idle and Infinity before manifestation are connected descriptions in the current framework. Zero is not another name for origination or for a manifested thing.

Open questions: What precisely characterizes the idle state, and how should the distinction between before manifestation and manifestation be understood without assuming chronology?

Zero — Idle State of Origin

Idle

Working meaning: Idle refers to the state of Origin represented by Zero, associated with Infinity before manifestation. In the developing understanding of existence and intelligence, idle is also described as the other half of the nature of existence and half of the source of intelligence.

Distinctions: Idle should not be reduced to absence, incapability, lack of freedom or inactivity within a manifested system. Nor should non-expression automatically be interpreted as intentional non-manifestation.

Open questions: The relationship between the idle state of Origin, the idle aspect of existence, choice, intelligence and non-manifestation requires continued examination. These uses are related, but their exact scope should not be presumed identical without further clarification.

3. Freewill and Choice

Freewill

Working meaning: Freewill is the functional perspective of Origin. It is an approximate description of the Source through its functionality, not an exhaustive definition of Origin.

Distinctions: Freewill should not automatically be restricted to familiar human ideas of personal decision-making. The framework considers it more broadly in relation to intrinsic originating capability and freedom of choice.

Relationship: Will is associated with Freewill. Intention is an expression of Will that may give a particular origination its direction or character. Experience may influence later intentions and expressions without creating, increasing or diminishing the underlying capability.

Open questions: How Freewill, choice, intention, actualization and the idle state relate remains open to investigation.

Consciousness and Freewill · Core Principles of Origin

Choice

Working meaning: Choice is treated as inherent freedom and as preceding origination. It includes the possibility of choosing to originate and the possibility of choosing not to originate.

Distinctions: Choice is not limited to selecting between options that have already manifested. In the current observed understanding, the possibility of not originating is as relevant as the possibility of originating.

Relationship: Freedom of choice is identified as the root of intelligence. Choice is also related to expression or non-expression and manifestation or non-manifestation.

Open questions: How choice operates in relation to Origin, idle, intention and particular manifestations remains to be clarified. The absence of an observed expression does not by itself reveal whether a choice not to express was made.

4. Origination and Manifestation

Origination

Working meaning: Origination concerns the capability and process through which manifestations come about and may give rise to further manifestations. It is not limited to a single beginning or a one-time event.

Distinctions: Origin is the fundamental source; origination is the coming-about of a manifestation. A manifestation may be the immediate source or contributor to a further origination without becoming a separate ultimate Origin. Not every change or transformation is necessarily a new origination.

Related modes: The framework distinguishes spontaneous imagination, through which manifestations arise, from intentional imagination directed by intention within an existing manifestation. These should not be conflated with the original description of the Source's two modes of spontaneous creation: automatic and autonomous.

Open questions: Criteria for distinguishing origination, change, transformation and emergence, and the mechanisms of different origination pathways, remain subjects for investigation.

Origination · Spontaneous and Intentional Imagination

Non-origination

Working meaning: Non-origination refers to the possibility of not originating. In the current observed understanding, it is an equally valid possibility alongside origination.

Distinctions: Non-origination is not automatically the same as failure, inability, prevention or an unobserved event. Whether a particular case involves a choice not to originate must not be inferred solely from the absence of a manifestation.

Open questions: How non-origination relates to choice, idle, intention and the conditions of manifestation remains open.

Manifestation

Working meaning: Manifestations are what come about through origination. The current framework refers broadly to Things and Life Forms as manifestations.

Distinctions: Origin is the fundamental source; origination is the capability and process; manifestation is what comes about. A manifestation may change, transform, persist or cease. These descriptions should not be treated as interchangeable.

Open questions: The scope of the term and the criteria for identifying particular things or life forms as manifestations remain subjects for clarification and investigation.

Manifestations

Non-manifestation

Working meaning: Non-manifestation is an important subject of investigation alongside manifestation. It concerns the not-manifesting or non-expressing side of the framework and is not automatically classified as a manifestation.

Distinctions: Intentional non-manifestation should be distinguished from other forms of non-expression and from cases in which a manifestation has simply not been observed. Absence of observation alone does not establish the reason for absence.

Open questions: How non-manifestation relates to choice, idle, origination and the conditions of observation remains open to investigation.

5. Intelligence, Experience and Information

Intelligence

Working meaning: Intelligence concerns how well something can do a thing: the degree of capability or effectiveness in doing it.

Relationship: In the current observed understanding, freedom of choice is the root of intelligence; experience accumulates intelligence; information is a third element that contributes to intelligence.

Distinctions: Freedom of choice, experience and information are related to intelligence but are not interchangeable with intelligence itself. Experience accumulates intelligence; intelligence is not treated as the source that accumulates experience.

Open questions: How these elements interact, how intelligence should be examined across different kinds of existence, and what role idle plays as half of the source of intelligence remain open research subjects.

Experience

Working meaning: Experience is what is encountered through manifestation. In the current understanding, experience accumulates intelligence and may influence subsequent intentions and expressions.

Distinctions: Experience is not information alone, nor is it identical to intelligence. It may inform later intention without necessarily determining it; spontaneous intention need not be determined by prior experience.

Open questions: The nature, accumulation and contribution of experience to intelligence remain subjects for further investigation.

Information

Working meaning: Information is identified as a third element contributing to intelligence, alongside freedom of choice and experience.

Distinctions: Information should not be substituted for experience or intelligence. The current framework identifies its contribution but does not, in this initial terminology entry, prescribe a complete definition or mechanism.

Open questions: What constitutes information in this context, how it contributes to intelligence, and how that contribution may be examined remain open.

6. Consciousness and Research

Consciousness

Working perspective: In the current framework, Consciousness describes the nature of Origin from the perspective of attributes. This is a descriptive perspective, not a complete operational definition of Consciousness.

Distinctions: Consciousness and Freewill are described as two perspectives on the same underlying nature of Origin. Their descriptive roles remain distinct: Consciousness concerns attributes, while Freewill concerns functionality.

Open questions: The full scope and operational description of Consciousness remain open to further research.

Consciousness and Freewill · Science of Consciousness

Freewill

Working perspective: Freewill describes the nature of Origin from the perspective of functionality. The current framework describes it in relation to freedom, including the freedom to change or not change, originate or not originate, manifest or not manifest, and transform or remain.

Distinctions: Freewill is related to Consciousness but is not an interchangeable definition for it. Earlier project materials organized Science of Freewill as a separate research field; the current public research page records the later clarification that it is not maintained as a separate science from Science of Consciousness.

Open questions: The relationships among Freewill, choice, intention, actualization and particular manifestations remain subjects for investigation.

What Is Freewill? · Consciousness and Freewill

Science of Consciousness

Working meaning: Science of Consciousness is a research undertaking within Science of Origin. It investigates the Nature of Life and the Nature of Things through the study of Consciousness and its Spectrum, using currently available methods while remaining open to further discoveries, perspectives and methodologies.

Distinctions: Science of Consciousness is the research undertaking; Spectrum / Spectra is a methodology used within the research; and the named spectra are subjects of investigation. These terms should not be treated as synonyms.

7. Spectrum and Measurement

Spectrum / Spectra

Working meaning: Spectrum / Spectra is the central methodology currently used in this research for measurable investigation. Spectrum refers to the entirety of frequencies within the relevant research domain, rather than only the portion currently detected. Spectra refers to multiple spectra or spectral representations used in comparative and related investigations.

Distinctions: The detectable portion of a Spectrum is affected by instrument capability, sensitivity and detection methods; it should not be mistaken for the entirety of the Spectrum. Spectrum / Spectra is a research methodology, not the same term as Science of Consciousness or any one research subject.

Open questions: Definitions, measurement methods and the relationships among particular spectra remain subjects for continued investigation.

Spectrum / Spectra: The Core Research Methodology

Spectrum of Consciousness

Working meaning: The complete spectrum of frequencies of Consciousness within the domain being investigated. The current framework also states that Spectrum of Freewill refers to the same Spectrum of Consciousness, describing it from a different perspective.

Distinctions: This term names a research subject; Spectrum / Spectra names the methodology. The relationship stated between Spectrum of Consciousness and Spectrum of Freewill does not establish an equivalence with Spectrum of Things.

Spectrum of Things

Working meaning: The complete spectrum of frequencies of things within the domain being investigated. Its research includes examining the properties and relationships of things through spectral investigation and measurement.

Research objective: Whether Spectrum of Things is equivalent to Spectrum of Consciousness remains an objective for investigation, not an established conclusion. It requires appropriate definitions, measurement methods, experimentation and reproducible evidence.

Strength of Consciousness

Working meaning: Strength of Consciousness is a project term associated with consciousness assessment and, in the current internal framework, an application of both Awakenology and Spectrum of Consciousness.

Historical source context: Earlier materials describe assessment points, testing procedures and instruments, composite Consciousness Strength levels, and interpretations relating those levels to union with Origin. These are distinct layers and should not be collapsed into one definition. The reviewed historical passages do not establish that all point collections, scales and numerical models belong to one unchanged system.

Limit: Historical source-reported levels, percentages and instrument descriptions are preserved as source claims; this reference does not present them as independently validated measurements. The selected English term is Strength of Consciousness, not “Consciousness Strength”.

8. Applications and Related Terms

Freedomality of Consciousness

Working meaning: In the current internal framework, Freedomality of Consciousness describes the degree to which an individual's inherent free will is actually expressed through their Soul Characteristics.

Limit: This is a framework concept and research direction. Its operational definition and measurement method require further development and validation. The related terms Soul Nature and Soul Characteristics also require fuller source review.

Freedomality Index (FI)

Working meaning: The Freedomality Index is described in the internal framework as a quantified representation or estimate of Freedomality of Consciousness.

Limit: Its scale, calculation, aggregation rules and interpretation remain to be operationally defined and validated. No fixed numerical scale is assumed.

Freedometry

Working meaning: Freedometry is a proposed measurement methodology for operationalizing and estimating Freedomality of Consciousness and its Freedomality Index.

Limit: It is a proposed methodology, not an established or validated measurement method. Direct observations must remain distinct from derived estimates and interpretations.

Entropy of Consciousness

Working meaning: Entropy of Consciousness is a research area examining entropy in relation to Consciousness, including its properties, characteristics and measurable aspects. The public research framework approaches it through multiple dimensions and does not reduce it to a single numerical value.

Limit: The reviewed sources do not provide one settled operational definition. A conventional thermodynamic definition should not be imported without a source-grounded mapping.

Entropy of Consciousness research

Strength of Consciousness of Things

Working meaning: This term names research into the strength of consciousness in relation to things, including its properties and potential measurement.

Distinctions: Keep it separate from Strength of Consciousness. The reviewed sources do not establish that the two terms name the same construct or that one is a renamed version of the other.

Awakenology

Working meaning: Awakenology is an application of Science of Consciousness and an umbrella framework encompassing both 催醒法 (methods and practice) and 催醒學 (knowledge and principles).

Distinctions: Awakenology is not limited to the practical method component alone. Its scope and relationships with other applications have been described differently across source versions; the current internal framework treats it as an umbrella framework.

Exincarnology

Working meaning: Exincarnology is the specialized discipline and practical framework concerning 脫離輪迴. In the current internal framework, it is the first application of Awakenology and encompasses both 脫離輪迴學 and 脫離輪迴法.

Distinctions: Exincarnology names the discipline and framework; Exincarnation names the process or achievement it aims at.

Exincarnation

Working meaning: Exincarnation refers to 脫離輪迴, the process or achievement associated with liberation from the reincarnation cycle in the current project terminology.

Distinctions: A historical source presents union with Origin and exiting the reincarnation cycle as distinct components of 得道. Exincarnation should therefore not be treated as a synonym for Union with Origin. This terminology entry records the project's language and does not independently verify the claimed outcome or its mechanism.

Further development

This initial reference concentrates on foundational terms and relationships. It can grow to include terminology from the other research divisions, more specific concepts, observations, experimental methods and terms that emerge through future inquiry.

New distinctions may require new terms; existing terms may acquire broader, narrower or different meanings. Where a term changes, its earlier usage may remain relevant to understanding the development of the framework. Revision should clarify the evolution of understanding rather than imply that the present wording was always final.
