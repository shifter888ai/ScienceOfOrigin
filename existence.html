II · Nature of Existence

Nature of Existence

Investigating the nature and forms of existence, including things, life, energy, matter, information, space and time.

Scope

This division investigates what existence appears to contain and the nature of its different forms. The subjects are not assumed to be independent fundamentals; their status and relationships remain matters for investigation.

Existing research subjects

Existence & Reality

The nature of existence, manifestation and reality.

 Things & Life

The broad relationship between Things, Life Forms and manifestation.

 Nature of Things

What Things are, their properties, forms, relationships and place among manifestations.

 Nature of Life

Life Forms, their distinguishing properties and their possible relationships with Consciousness and Freewill.

 Energy & Matter

The nature and relationship of energy and matter.

 Information & Patterns

Information, patterns and their roles in manifestations and systems.

 Space & Time

The nature of space and time within and across manifestations.
