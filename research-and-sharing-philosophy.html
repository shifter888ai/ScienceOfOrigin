Research and Sharing Philosophy

Research, Discoveries and Independent Investigation

Science of Origin prioritizes the research itself: observation, experimentation, investigation and discovery. Sharing discoveries is part of this work; convincing others is not its purpose.

1. Research Comes First

The primary focus is conducting research and making discoveries. Time and effort are directed toward observation, experimentation, investigation, analysis and further research.

Research activity should not be displaced by unnecessary work whose purpose is to persuade others to accept a discovery.

2. Sharing Discoveries

Discoveries are shared so that others may encounter them and explore the subjects independently.

Sharing is an act of making discoveries available. It is not a commitment to persuade every reader, obtain agreement, or manage every response.

3. Original Research Materials

Original research materials and records are preserved by the researcher. Their preservation does not create an obligation to publish or disclose them.

The researcher decides what to share. The discoveries selected for sharing may be published without making the complete collection of original materials available.

4. Independent Observation and Experimentation

Readers who do not accept or agree with a discovery are free to conduct their own observations and experiments, draw their own conclusions, and pursue their own discoveries.

Independent investigation allows each researcher to examine a subject directly rather than relying on another person to do the work on their behalf.

5. No Obligation to Convince or Supervise

Sharing a discovery does not create an obligation to convince others, repeatedly defend their acceptance, provide additional materials on demand, or supervise their independent research.

Researchers are not responsible for tutoring or babysitting other researchers. Each person may undertake their own investigation and reach their own understanding.

6. Share and Move Forward

The approach is straightforward: conduct the research, share the discoveries chosen for publication, and continue with the next research work.

Research remains active and ongoing. Time spent on unnecessary convincing or supervision is time taken away from further observation, experimentation and discovery.

Ongoing Research

This philosophy keeps the focus on research and discovery while leaving readers free to examine, investigate and understand for themselves. Sharing does not close inquiry, and it does not transfer responsibility for another person's research to the discoverer.
